✨UPDATE✨ SG Bus Timings 2.4.8
sgbus.uwuapps.org

New:
- GTA VI colours: a theme in the game's logo colours, yours to keep if you set it before 26 November
- The magic cup: changing language takes a sip from Singapore's famous language-changing cup
- uwuCoins History: every coin in and out, and where it came from
- Simple (Lite), Normal and Advanced Super App: new names for how much to start with

Website and desktop app:
- Phone's setting in Theme: light or dark follows your device

Desktop app:
- Make it yours: language, start at sign-in, light or dark and colour, all in one window
- Install and update with apt on Debian, Ubuntu, Mint and Raspberry Pi OS

Android app:
- Your whole trip as a bar in its notification, a Live Update on Android 16 and later
- The trip's timeline: every stop on the way, a tap on the trip bar
- The Get Off Alert buzzes OFF in Morse code, and starts from a bus's route
- Trips follow you more closely, and move on to your next ride by themselves
- Through tunnels, your dot keeps going until the GPS is back
- Off the route? Directions again from where you are
- Last bus gone or a faster way? The trip finds it and says why
- Across the Causeway: an alert at each checkpoint
- Previous and next buttons in the small window
- Navigate plans with no connection, and reads pasted Google Maps links
- Set Home and Work from Navigate, and Citymapper from a stop's Directions
- Your trips by the week, with your waits and rides, Plan again and Share
- Find a setting: search Settings by name or by what it does
- The map follows you properly, with a handle to tilt and turn, and your speed
- Tap a stop or station in Explorer to open its card
- Nothing runs under Android's buttons any more
- Long words fit in every language, Tamil included
- A smaller app

Guide:
- The Android app has its own section, with pictures of the app
