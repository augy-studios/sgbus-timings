Bus numbers you can read in dark mode, writing that meets WCAG's contrast on every colour, and live timings in the colour of how full each bus is on the website.

## Download

| Device | File |
|---|---|
| Android 7.0 or newer | `sg-bus-timings-2.6.1.apk` |
| Wear OS 3 or newer watch | `sg-bus-timings-2.6.1-watch.apk` |
| Windows 10 or 11 | `sg-bus-timings-2.6.1-setup.exe` (or the `.msi`) |
| Mac with Apple silicon, or Intel | `sg-bus-timings-2.6.1-mac-arm64.dmg`, or `-mac-x64.dmg` |
| Debian, Ubuntu, Mint; Fedora, openSUSE, RHEL | the `.deb`; the `.rpm` |
| Any other Linux (with GTK 3 and WebKitGTK 4.1) | the `.AppImage` |

The Linux files need Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12 or newer.

The desktop files join the release a few minutes after it's out. Windows SmartScreen: More info > Run anyway. macOS, the first time: System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway.

## Updating

**Android**: tap **Update** on the bar at the top of the app, or the download-arrow button in the Edge Tray.

**Windows (`.exe`), Mac and AppImage**: the app updates itself. **Windows (`.msi`), `.deb` and `.rpm`**: a notification says 2.6.1 is out; install the new one over the old one.

**Wear OS**: nothing has changed on the watch, so there's no hurry to install 2.6.1 on it.

Your favourites and settings stay when you update.

## What's new

### Website, Android and desktop apps
- **Bus numbers you can read in dark mode**: in Navigate's live timings, the bus numbers were white on white. The sort button you've picked and the trip bar's current part on the website had the same trouble.
- **Easier to read on every colour**: station codes, door labels, and the map's Board, Change and Get off bubbles are black on light colours such as the Circle Line's orange, not white. The Your bus tag, Explorer's bus buttons and the door labels in dark mode are clearer too. All the writing now meets WCAG's AA contrast.

### Website
- **Live timings in colour**: Navigate's live timings are green, amber or red for how full each bus is, as the guide says. They were all grey. Screen readers also say how full each bus is.

### Linux command line
- No changes of its own: numbered 2.6.1 to stay in step with the apps.
