✨UPDATE✨ SG Bus Timings 2.3.0
sgbus.uwuapps.org

New:
- Shuttle buses all over the island: IKEA, Changi Airport, KKH, Mandai, Clarke Quay, the TS1 to JB and more, on Bus timings, Explorer and Navigate
- Navigate rides NUS and NTU campus shuttles too
- Some shuttles show their next bus live
- Free or paid shuttles, your pick in Settings
- uwuCoins for your trips, and a wheel to spin them on, like gacha
- Achievements in Settings: see how far along each one is
- Easter eggs, and the secrets they unlock 👀
- Glance and Favourites show the same list
- Are you walking? On a trip, switch to cycling or driving directions
- Drive there in Navigate

Android app:
- A new secret for Android 12 and later, for whoever finds it
