The guide now has a section of its own for the Android app, and the app's Guide button opens it.

## Download

| Device | File |
|---|---|
| Android 7.0 or newer | `sg-bus-timings-2.4.4.apk` |
| Wear OS 3 or newer watch | `sg-bus-timings-2.4.4-watch.apk` |

This update is for the Android app. The desktop apps and the command line stay on 2.4.0.

## Updating

**Android**: tap **Update** on the bar at the top of the app, or the download-arrow button in the Edge Tray. New to the app? Download the `.apk` on your phone and open it.

**Wear OS**: nothing has changed on the watch, so there's no hurry to install 2.4.4 on it.

Your favourites and settings stay when you update.

## What's new

- **The app's own guide**: [the Android app's section](https://guide.sgbus.uwuapps.org/android/overview) describes and shows the app's own screens. The **Guide** button in the Edge Tray and Settings opens it.
- **The departures board**: its page number is no longer cut off at the foot of the screen on a phone held upright.
