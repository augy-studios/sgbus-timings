GTA VI colours for the game's release, previous and next buttons in the small window on a trip, and the app now follows a trip as closely as the website does.

## Download

| Device | File |
|---|---|
| Android 7.0 or newer | `sg-bus-timings-2.4.6.apk` |
| Wear OS 3 or newer watch | `sg-bus-timings-2.4.6-watch.apk` |

This update is for the Android app. The desktop apps and the command line stay on 2.4.0.

## Updating

**Android**: tap **Update** on the bar at the top of the app, or the download-arrow button in the Edge Tray. New to the app? Download the `.apk` on your phone and open it.

**Wear OS**: nothing has changed on the watch, so there's no hurry to install 2.4.6 on it.

Your favourites and settings stay when you update.

## What's new

### GTA VI colours
- **A theme for the game's release on 19 November**: its logo's pink, blue, orange, yellow and violet, with a colour to each kind of thing, and a violet page in dark mode. A window offers it the first time you open this version, or in Theme, pick **GTA VI** under **Colours** in Material You, or under **Brand colour** in the UwU Apps theme. It's on the website too. SG Bus Timings isn't connected with Rockstar Games.
- **Set it before 26 November to keep it**: set it at least once by then and it's yours for good, even if you change colours later. After that, it has to be won on the uwuCoins wheel: the egg slice wins it first.

### Picture-in-picture
- **Previous and next**: on a trip by bus or train with more than one part, tap the small window for previous and next buttons, as on a video, to move between parts.
- **Next can't end your trip**: on the last part, Next is grey, on the trip bar and in the small window.

### Trips
- **On to your next ride by itself**: if your location shows you already on the next bus or train, the trip moves on to it, even if it never saw you reach the end of the part before.
- **Follows you more closely**: waiting at the stop no longer counts as riding, and with no GPS the trip holds until your bus is due. It follows the bus's real road, puts itself right when your location shows it's wrong, and ignores a stray location further on than a bus could have gone.
- **Back on the trip bar**: goes back to where you'd got to on that part, not its start.
- **Back to Navigate**: opens the way you picked, not the list of ways.
- **Across the Causeway**: a ride across is followed in parts, so the alert tells you to get off at each checkpoint.

### Navigate and more
- **Citymapper**: a stop's Directions opens into Live map and Citymapper, and a walk in Navigate has both.
- **Navigate's tabs keep your ends**: a place becomes the bus stop at it, or the nearest within 150 m, and the time comes too.
- **Set Home and Work from Navigate**: until they're set, their buttons show paler under each end, and tapping one asks where it is.
- **Share on Bus only**: sends a link to the route, with the time if you picked one.
- **Directions there**: on someone's live progress, plans your own way to where they're going.
- **To Home**: a trip in Your trips that ended at Home, Work or one of your places says so.
- **Clear of the navigation bar, everywhere**: Glance, Bus timings, Favourites, Navigate, Service alerts and Places no longer run under Android's buttons.
