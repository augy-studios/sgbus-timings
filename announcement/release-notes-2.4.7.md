Location messages now follow your language, and Glance fits better in Tamil.

## Download

| Device | File |
|---|---|
| Android 7.0 or newer | `sg-bus-timings-2.4.7.apk` |
| Wear OS 3 or newer watch | `sg-bus-timings-2.4.7-watch.apk` |

This update is for the Android app. The desktop apps and the command line stay on 2.4.0.

## Updating

**Android**: tap **Update** on the bar at the top of the app, or the download-arrow button in the Edge Tray. New to the app? Download the `.apk` on your phone and open it.

**Wear OS**: nothing has changed on the watch, so there's no hurry to install 2.4.7 on it.

Your favourites and settings stay when you update.

## What's new

- **Location messages follow your language**: switching language and back no longer leaves "Couldn't get your location" in the other language, in Bus timings, Glance or Navigate.
- **Arriving fits in Tamil**: on Glance, the big "Arriving" gets smaller rather than breaking in the middle of the word.
