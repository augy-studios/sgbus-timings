✨UPDATE✨ SG Bus Timings 2.6.0
sgbus.uwuapps.org

Website, apps and Telegram bot:
- Navigate sorts its ways: Best, Fastest, Least walking or Fewest changes
- Each way's fare by card, for your fare type
- The buses you'd catch, live, with how full each is
- Your buses: preferred buses, and the ones you take to each place, come first
- A spin of the uwuCoins wheel costs 50, not 100

Trips, on the website and apps:
- A strip under the trip bar: the part you're on, stop by stop
- Bubbles on the map: where to board, change and get off
- On a bus or a train, you're shown as one

Android app:
- Live Updates on Android 16: your trip at the top of your notifications and on the lock screen

Linux command line:
- sgbus nav sorts and prices its ways too

Download the apps and view patch notes: github.com/augy-studios/sgbus-timings/releases/latest
