uwuCoins, feats and team runs come to the Telegram bot, and the website and the app open its Easter egg links and sync uwuCoins with it.

## Download

| Device | File |
|---|---|
| Android 7.0 or newer | `sg-bus-timings-2.4.11.apk` |
| Wear OS 3 or newer watch | `sg-bus-timings-2.4.11-watch.apk` |

This update is for the Android app. The desktop apps and the command line stay on 2.4.8: the desktop apps get the website's side of this by themselves.

## Updating

**Android**: tap **Update** on the bar at the top of the app, or the download-arrow button in the Edge Tray. New to the app? Download the `.apk` on your phone and open it.

**Wear OS**: nothing has changed on the watch, so there's no hurry to install 2.4.11 on it.

Your favourites and settings stay when you update.

## What's new

### Android app
- **Easter egg links**: the Telegram bot's wheel gives its egg as a link. Tap it and the app opens it for the egg slice's prize: GTA VI's colours once they're the wheel's to give, otherwise an egg you haven't found, or 80 uwuCoins. Each link works once.
- **uwuCoins in sync**: sync offers your uwuCoins, and taking another phone's or the bot's adds them to yours.
- **Join in Telegram**: a team run has Join in Telegram, for the same team in the bot.

### Telegram bot ([@sgbus_app](https://t.me/sgbus_app))
- **uwuCoins** for live trips with a real ride, and **/coins**, the wheel, spinning in the chat.
- **Feats** with **/achievements**, and **team runs** with **/team**, phones and Telegram chats together.
- **A map along the way** on a live trip, with each bus in blue and each train in its line's colour.
