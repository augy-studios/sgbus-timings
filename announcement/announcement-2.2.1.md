✨UPDATE✨ SG Bus Timings 2.2.1
sgbus.uwuapps.org

New:
- MyICA on the trip bar: on a trip to Tuas or Woodlands Checkpoint, one tap for your immigration QR code
- Search a stop's name and road together: Blk 109 Bedok finds just the Blk 109s on Bedok's roads
- Search understands LTA's short names: Bukit Batok finds Bt Batok Int, Opposite finds Opp
- Type Bedok MRT or Senja LRT for the bus stops at its exits
- Loop buses like 73 are listed once each way round, in their own colours
- Woodlands Checkpoint and the stops in JB marked, with a passport reminder

Android app:
- Routines and Timings near a stop get their own Edge Tray buttons

Download the apps and view patch notes: github.com/augy-studios/sgbus-timings/releases/latest
