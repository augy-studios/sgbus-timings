## Updating

**Android**: tap **Update** on the bar at the top of the app, or the download-arrow button in the Edge Tray. New to the app? Download the `.apk` on your phone and open it. If Play Protect asks about the app, tap **More details** > **Install anyway**. Almost everything below is already in the app you have, as it comes from the website; 2.3.0 adds something for Android 12 and later, for whoever finds it.

**Wear OS**: nothing has changed on the watch in this version, so there's no need to install 2.3.0 on it.

**Windows (`.exe`), Mac and AppImage**: the app checks as it opens and every hour, downloads the update, and offers **Restart to update**. To get it sooner, right-click the tray icon and pick **Check for updates...**.

**Windows (`.msi`) and Debian (`.deb`)**: a notification says 2.3.0 is out, unless **New versions of the app** is off. Download the new installer and install it over the old one.

**Command line for Linux**: the single file updates itself with `sgbus update`. From the `.deb`, download the new one and `sudo apt install ./sg-bus-timings-cli-2.3.0-all.deb`. Nothing has changed in it in this version, so there's no hurry.

Your favourites and settings stay when you update.

## What's new

### Shuttle buses
- **Shuttles all over the island**: NUS's and NTU's campus shuttles, and the ones anyone can ride: IKEA Tampines', Changi Airport's between the terminals and to Tanah Merah Ferry Terminal, KK Women's and Children's Hospital's, Mandai's, Clarke Quay's late-night shuttles home, Orchid Country Club's and the TS1 to Johor Bahru. Each from its operator's own timetable.
- **In Navigate**: Bus and train rides them too, with each shuttle's average wait from its timetable, so a way can start at PGP or end at IKEA. A hotel's shuttle is only used to or from that hotel.
- **Free or paid**: **Settings** > **Navigate** > **Shuttle buses**: **Free only**, the default, or **Free and paid** for Mandai's, the ferry terminal shuttle and the TS1 too. Explorer follows it.
- **Search them**: type a route, such as A1, CL-B or M2, for its stops and timetable, or a stop only shuttles use, such as PGP or IKEA Tampines, for its shuttle times.
- **Live where they can be**: IKEA's and the Novotel and Mercure on Stevens' shuttles show their next bus live.

### uwuCoins
- **Coins for your trips**: trips taken with the Get Off Alert to the end earn uwuCoins, more for longer trips, changes, and each bus and train operator and line. The coin in the Edge Tray shows how many you have.
- **The wheel**: in **Settings** > **Help and extras**, like gacha: 100 coins a spin, and every spin wins an Easter egg or more coins. Eggs bring secrets.
- **Achievements**: a new group in **Settings** lists the feats, each worth 100 coins once, with how far along you are, and the Easter eggs you've found.

### Themes
- **Your colours from one colour**: Your colours, a secret an egg unlocks, takes one colour, picked on a colour wheel or from presets, and makes the whole theme from it, light and dark, with every bit of text easy to read.

### Bus timings
- **Glance and Favourites in step**: pick a list on Glance and Favourites opens on it, and the other way round.
- **The greeting**: tapping it no longer opens Settings.

### Navigate and trips
- **Are you walking?**: on a trip, a walk that goes faster than anyone walks asks whether you're cycling or driving, and switches the way to it, with a reminder to ride safely and each bus or train ahead to ride to instead.
- **Drive there**: under Walk there and Cycle there, how long driving the whole way takes.
- **Calendar in Add a stop**, and calendar events listed until they end.

### Explorer
- **Layers**: a button on Explorer turns bus stops, MRT and LRT stations and lines, and the shuttles' routes and stops off or on.

### Android app
- **Every version in Updates**: the update page lists every version back to 1.0.0.
