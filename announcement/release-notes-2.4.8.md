## Updating

**Android**: tap **Update** on the bar at the top of the app, or the download-arrow button in the Edge Tray. New to the app? Download the `.apk` on your phone and open it.

**Wear OS**: nothing has changed on the watch, so there's no hurry to install 2.4.8 on it.

**Windows (`.exe`), Mac and AppImage**: the app checks as it opens and every hour, downloads the update, and offers **Restart to update**. To get it sooner, right-click the tray icon and pick **Check for updates...**.

**Windows (`.msi`), `.deb` and `.rpm`**: a notification says 2.4.8 is out, unless **New versions of the app** is off. Download the new installer and install it over the old one.

**Command line for Linux**: the single file updates itself with `sgbus update`. From the `.deb`, download the new one and `sudo apt install ./sg-bus-timings-cli-2.4.8-all.deb`. Nothing has changed in it, so there's no hurry.

Your favourites and settings stay when you update.

## What's new

### Desktop apps
- **Make it yours**: the first time 2.4.8 opens, after installing or updating, a window sets your language, whether it starts when you sign in, light or dark, and your brand colour, all in one place. It's in the tray's menu whenever you want it again.

### Website and desktop apps
- **Phone's setting**: a fourth choice under **Mode** in Theme, as the Android app has, following your device's light or dark as it changes.
- **Asked in every language**: new visitors whose browser isn't in English are now asked how much to start with, as everyone else is.
- **GTA VI's Edge Tray**: the page you're on now reads clearly, its icon dark on orange.

### Everywhere
- **New names for the first open**: Minimal, Standard and Full are now **Simple (Lite)**, **Normal** and **Advanced Super App**.
- **The magic cup**: changing the language now takes a sip from Singapore's famous language-changing cup. It spins in 3D with the tea swishing around it, for about a second, and the whole app comes back in the new language.

### Everywhere with uwuCoins (website, desktop and Android)
- **uwuCoins history**: under the wheel, **History** lists every coin in and out and where it came from: which trip and what made up its coins, which feat, each spin and what it won, with **All**, **Earned** and **Spent**. The details start from this version: older entries show what was kept at the time.

### Android app
- **A stop's bus cards**: there's a gap between the buses, a long time goes onto a second line only between its minutes and seconds, and a long wheelchair label wraps rather than running into the next bus, in every language.
- **Choices that fit**: where a language's words don't fit side by side, as in Tamil, choices such as Service alerts' and Settings' stack one under another rather than breaking in the middle of a word.
