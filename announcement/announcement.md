✨UPDATE✨ SG Bus Timings
sgbus.uwuapps.org
New:
- Direct bus hints: you will now be able to tell if a route you're planning has a direct bus. Type a stop, use your location or a postal code for one end, and the stops with a bus straight to the other end come first, with the bus numbers (screenshot attached)
- Navigate: get from any address, building, postal code, station or stop to any other by bus and train. Every worthwhile mix is listed side by side, like 1 train then 2 buses, or 2 trains then 1 bus, not just the fastest
- Get Off Alert: start a trip and your phone buzzes in short pulses two stops before your change or your stop. On by default
- Service alerts: MRT/LRT disruptions and traffic incidents as notifications, even with the app closed. Pick train and traffic alerts separately, all updates or disruptions only
- Weather at a glance: today's temperature, PSI and PM2.5 ranges in one button, with today's forecast and air quality by region a tap away
