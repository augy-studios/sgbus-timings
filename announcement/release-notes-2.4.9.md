Fixes for tablets and phones on their side, confetti that clears, Add a stop between the ends, and traffic on the map that shows you where it is.

## Download

| Device | File |
|---|---|
| Android 7.0 or newer | `sg-bus-timings-2.4.9.apk` |
| Wear OS 3 or newer watch | `sg-bus-timings-2.4.9-watch.apk` |

This update is for the Android app. The desktop apps and the command line stay on 2.4.8.

## Updating

**Android**: tap **Update** on the bar at the top of the app, or the download-arrow button in the Edge Tray. New to the app? Download the `.apk` on your phone and open it.

**Wear OS**: nothing has changed on the watch, so there's no hurry to install 2.4.9 on it.

Your favourites and settings stay when you update.

## What's new

### Everywhere
- **Straight to the traffic**: **See it on the map**, under a ride with traffic in Navigate or on a trip, closes in on the jammed and slow stretches and any incidents once LTA's traffic is in. In the Android app it used to open on all of Singapore instead of the ride.

### Android app
- **Phones on their side**: the Edge Tray keeps clear of the navigation bar and the camera, and the language list scrolls down to Bahasa Melayu and Tamil. Glance's choice of stops, Explorer's Layers, Share, Pick a bus, Change here and saving the map scroll too when they don't fit.
- **Tours you can see**: a page's tour scrolls to what it's showing you, as Service alerts' Notify me further down, and keeps its card clear of the status bar, the navigation bar and a tablet's taskbar. On a tablet the card sits under what it points at.
- **Add a stop between the ends**: in Navigate, Add a stop sits between From and To, where the stop comes in the journey.
- **Confetti that clears**: after a spin or a celebration, all the confetti falls off the screen and fades away, instead of some of it stopping halfway and staying.
- **Doors that fit**: where a change has more doors than fit beside their words, as at Bishan for the Circle Line, they go onto a second line instead of running off the edge.
