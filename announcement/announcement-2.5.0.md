✨UPDATE✨ SG Bus Timings 2.5.0
sgbus.uwuapps.org

Desktop app:
- Rebuilt as an app of its own, with the Android app's screens and everything you'd saved
- A command bar: Ctrl+K (⌘K on a Mac) finds any stop, bus or command
- Keyboard shortcuts, and the Mac's menu bar
- Made for a big window: a stop or bus opens beside your list
- Your computer's own look: Fluent 2 on Windows, macOS Tahoe, and Frosted Glass on Linux
- Right-click menus on stops, buses and favourites

Android app:
- A shorter trip bar and note
- Your favourite buses first in Pick a bus
- Your trip's notification with your others, not under Silent

Website and Android app:
- Navigate never offers a bus that will have finished for the day
- GTA VI colours: set them by 14 December to keep them
- Causeway Link's buses in Bus timings

Download the apps and view patch notes: github.com/augy-studios/sgbus-timings/releases/latest
