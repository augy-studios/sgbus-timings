Tapping a bus stop or a station in Explorer works again, Navigate has more room, and pages no longer run under Android's buttons.

## Download

| Device | File |
|---|---|
| Android 7.0 or newer | `sg-bus-timings-2.4.3.apk` |
| Wear OS 3 or newer watch | `sg-bus-timings-2.4.3-watch.apk` |

This update is for the Android app. The desktop apps and the command line stay on 2.4.0.

## Updating

**Android**: tap **Update** on the bar at the top of the app, or the download-arrow button in the Edge Tray. New to the app? Download the `.apk` on your phone and open it.

**Wear OS**: nothing has changed on the watch, so there's no hurry to install 2.4.3 on it.

Your favourites and settings stay when you update.

## What's fixed

### Explorer
- **Tap a stop or station**: tapping a bus stop or an MRT or LRT station did nothing. Its card now opens on the dot, as on the website. The stops and stations on a trip or a walk open theirs too.
- **Easier to tap**: a tap counts within a finger's width of a dot, and goes to a station first, then the stop nearest it.
- **Back where you left it**: leave Explorer by a card's **Show bus timings** or **Directions**, and it opens on that stop or station again, card and all. Close it with its back arrow and it opens on you.
- **Show bus timings**: it now closes the map, and offline the button is hidden, as Bus timings needs a connection.

### Navigate
- **More room**: the swap button now sits between **From** and **To**, on **Bus only** too, so the boxes and your places get the full width.

### Everywhere
- **Clear of the navigation bar**: Settings and other pages, and the sheets that pull up, no longer run under Android's buttons at the bottom of the screen.
