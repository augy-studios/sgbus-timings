The desktop app rebuilt as an app of its own, with the Android app's screens, a command bar, keyboard shortcuts and your computer's own look, and every platform on 2.5.0.

## Download

| Device | File |
|---|---|
| Android 7.0 or newer | `sg-bus-timings-2.5.0.apk` |
| Wear OS 3 or newer watch | `sg-bus-timings-2.5.0-watch.apk` |
| Windows 10 or 11 | `sg-bus-timings-2.5.0-setup.exe` (or the `.msi`) |
| Mac with Apple silicon, or Intel | `sg-bus-timings-2.5.0-mac-arm64.dmg`, or `-mac-x64.dmg` |
| Debian, Ubuntu, Mint; Fedora, openSUSE, RHEL | the `.deb`; the `.rpm` |
| Any other Linux (with GTK 3 and WebKitGTK 4.1) | the `.AppImage` |
| Linux command line | `sg-bus-timings-cli-2.5.0-all.deb`, or `sgbus` |

The Linux files need Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12 or newer.

The desktop files join the release a few minutes after it's out. Windows SmartScreen: More info > Run anyway. macOS, the first time: System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway.

## Updating

**Android**: tap **Update** on the bar at the top of the app, or the download-arrow button in the Edge Tray.

**Windows (`.exe`), Mac and AppImage**: the app updates itself, the old desktop app too. **Windows (`.msi`), `.deb` and `.rpm`**: a notification says 2.5.0 is out; install the new one over the old one.

**Wear OS**: nothing has changed on the watch, so there's no hurry to install 2.5.0 on it.

Your favourites and settings stay when you update.

## What's new

### Desktop app
- **An app of its own**: the Android app's screens, laid out for a computer, with everything you'd saved brought across the first time it opens.
- **A command bar**: Ctrl+K (⌘K on a Mac) finds any stop, bus or command, and opens a pasted link.
- **Keyboard shortcuts**: Back and Forward, Reload, Settings, full screen and more, with ⌘ on a Mac and the Mac's menu bar.
- **Made for a big window**: a navigation rail, and a stop or bus opening beside your list. A narrow window is laid out as the phone is.
- **Your computer's own look**: Fluent 2 (Acrylic & Mica) on Windows, macOS Tahoe on a Mac, and Deepin & COSMIC Frosted Glass on Linux, in your computer's accent colour.
- **Right-click menus, dialogs and a hand over buttons**: on stops, buses and favourites; pickers in the middle of the window; and the tray's menu closes when you click outside it.

### Android app
- **A shorter trip bar and note**: a line for where you're going, two for the part you're on, and a note of two lines at most, with no gap under the bars.
- **Your buses first in Pick a bus**: your favourite buses at the top, each with a star.
- **Your trip with your other notifications**: not under Silent, with one sound as the trip starts.
- **No crash coming back** after a long time in the background, and Start trip within reach on a phone.

### Website and Android app
- **No way with a bus gone for the day**: Navigate, and the Telegram bot's /nav, never offer a bus that will have finished by the time you'd reach it.
- **GTA VI colours until 14 December**: set them once by then and they're yours to keep.
- **Causeway Link in Bus timings**, and Tuas Checkpoint's stop says which buses cross instead of on foot.

### Linux command line
- No changes of its own: numbered 2.5.0 to stay in step with the apps.
