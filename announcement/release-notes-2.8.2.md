The desktop files join the release a few minutes after it's out. Windows SmartScreen: More info > Run anyway. macOS, the first time: System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway.

## Updating

**Android**: tap **Update** on the bar at the top of the app, or the download-arrow button in the Edge Tray.

**Windows (`.exe`), Mac and AppImage**: the app updates itself. **Windows (`.msi`), `.deb` and `.rpm`**: a notification says 2.8.2 is out; install the new one over the old one.

**Wear OS**: nothing has changed on the watch, so there's no hurry to install 2.8.2 on it.

Your favourites and settings stay when you update.

## What's new

### Themes
- **IKEA colours**: IKEA's blue and yellow, to see off the free shuttle to IKEA Tampines, which runs for the last time on 31 Dec 2026. Navy words on a pale blue page (deep navy in dark mode), yellow for what's picked, and blue main buttons with yellow words.
- **Yours to keep**: set it at least once by 31 Dec 2026, Singapore time, and it's yours forever. After that it's won on the uwuCoins wheel. A window offers it once when the app opens.
- **Where to find it**: last of the brand colours in Theme, and under Colours in the Android and desktop apps' Material You.
- **GTA VI - Vice City**: GTA VI's colours now have the full name in the brand colours.

### uwuCoins
- **75 coins a spin**: one price for the wheel on the website, the apps and the Telegram bot.
- **Event colours on the wheel**: if you didn't set GTA VI's colours by 14 December, or IKEA's by 31 December, the egg slice wins them first, GTA VI's then IKEA's.
- **Event colours found**: under the wheel and in Achievements, with the feats and Easter eggs.

### Android app
- **A train's bar on a trip**: once your location has you along a train ride, the trip's bar and time left have you on the train, not still waiting at the start.
- **Where you get off, at the end of the bar**: the stop's name sits under the bar's right-hand end.

### Telegram bot
- **Spin for 75**: the bot's wheel costs the same as the app's, and its egg link can give GTA VI's or IKEA's colours while the wheel has them to give.
