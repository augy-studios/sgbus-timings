✨UPDATE✨ SG Bus Timings 2.3.1
sgbus.uwuapps.org

New:
- The nearest exit: Navigate says which station exit to use, such as Exit F at Tampines
- Saved navs keep their stop on the way, on the web and in the Telegram bot
- Train doors fold away in Navigate, a tap to open
- The whole app in Chinese, Malay and Tamil, not just the buttons
- SGH's campus shuttles, and shuttle routes drawn along their roads
- Coins for your past trips, once
- Share your feats as a card with a link
- Three new feats, and a switch to hide uwuCoins
- Trips follow your location more closely, and CW or ACW on the Circle Line
- Report a bug: pictures, the whole page, and an ID for each report

Android app:
- Pick the app's language in Android's own settings, on Android 13 and later
- Notifications, shortcuts, tiles and the widget in your language
- Another secret, for whoever finds it 👀
