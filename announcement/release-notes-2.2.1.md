## Updating

**Android**: tap **Update** on the bar at the top of the app, or the download-arrow button in the Edge Tray. New to the app? Download the `.apk` on your phone and open it. If Play Protect asks about the app, tap **More details** > **Install anyway**. Only the MyICA button opening MyICA itself needs this version: everything else below is already in the app.

**Wear OS**: install the new `-watch.apk` the same way as before, from a computer (see the guide's Wear OS page). Nothing has changed on the watch in this version, so there's no hurry.

**Windows (`.exe`), Mac and AppImage**: the app downloads the update by itself within the hour, then a notification (unless **New versions of the app** is off in Service alerts), the tray's menu and the green button in the title bar offer **Restart to update**. If you don't restart, it updates the next time you quit. To get it sooner, right-click the tray icon and pick **Check for updates...**. Nothing has changed in the desktop app itself, so there's no hurry.

**Windows (`.msi`) and Debian (`.deb`)**: a notification says 2.2.1 is out, unless **New versions of the app** is off. Download the new installer and install it over the old one.

**New to the desktop app?**
- **Windows**: if Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info** > **Run anyway**.
- **Mac**: the first time, right-click the app in Finder > **Open** > **Open**, or on macOS 15 and later, **System Settings** > **Privacy & Security** > **Open Anyway**.
- **AppImage**: make it runnable (right-click > **Properties** > **Allow executing file as program**), then open it.

Your favourites and settings stay when you update.

## What's new

### Android app
- **MyICA, straight away**: on a trip to a checkpoint, the trip bar's **MyICA** button opens ICA's MyICA Mobile app itself, for your immigration QR code, as the SimplyGo button does SimplyGo. If it isn't installed, its Play Store page opens instead.
- **Routines and Timings near a stop in the Edge Tray**: both move out of Settings onto buttons of their own, a clock and a crosshair, each with its own window. Routines lists yours with **Add a routine** underneath, and stays open so you can set up several in a row. Either button can be turned off in **Settings** > **Edge Tray buttons**.

### Everywhere
- **MyICA at the checkpoint**: on a phone, a trip to Tuas or Woodlands Checkpoint (or Woodlands Train Checkpoint), or on across the Causeway, from the route planner or Navigate, has a **MyICA** button on the trip bar, for ICA's app and your immigration QR code. On an iPhone or iPad, or in a phone's browser, it opens MyICA Mobile's store page, which says **Open** once it's installed.
- **A stop's name and road together**: type them in any order, **Blk 109 Bedok**, and the list narrows to the Blk 109s on Bedok's roads rather than every Blk 109 in Singapore.
- **Shortened names**: type a word in full or as LTA shortens it, and search finds both: **Bukit Batok** finds Bt Batok Int, **Opposite Block 10** finds Opp Blk 10, and Ctr and Ctrl both find Centre and Central, among about 90 short forms. When some stops were only found that way, a line above them says so: **Also searched Bt for Bukit**.
- **Stops at a station**: type **Bedok MRT**, **Senja LRT** or **Botanic Gardens station** for the stops within about 250 m of its exits, nearest first.
- **Loops in two**: a loop bus passing a stop twice, such as 73, is listed once for each time round, on its way out and on its way back, each in its own colour, as a bus going both ways is. The departures board too.
- **Across the border**: Woodlands Checkpoint and the stops in Johor Bahru (its checkpoint, JB Sentral and Larkin Terminal) are marked, with a passport reminder, on the stop, in search results and in route lists.
