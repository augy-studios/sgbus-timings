The map keeps going through tunnels, finds the way again from where you are, and a drive on a trip gets a driving route. Glance also fits better on a narrow screen.

## Download

| Device | File |
|---|---|
| Android 7.0 or newer | `sg-bus-timings-2.4.5.apk` |
| Wear OS 3 or newer watch | `sg-bus-timings-2.4.5-watch.apk` |

This update is for the Android app. The desktop apps and the command line stay on 2.4.0.

## Updating

**Android**: tap **Update** on the bar at the top of the app, or the download-arrow button in the Edge Tray. New to the app? Download the `.apk` on your phone and open it.

**Wear OS**: nothing has changed on the watch, so there's no hurry to install 2.4.5 on it.

Your favourites and settings stay when you update.

## What's new

### The map
- **Through tunnels**: when the GPS stops, your dot keeps going along your route from how fast you were going, and says **No GPS here, so this is an estimate.** The phone's accelerometer tells when you've stopped in a jam, when you pull away, and when you brake. The next turn, distance and arrival time keep going with it, while the app is open, until the GPS is back.
- **A drive's own route on a trip**: a trip switched to driving or cycling shows the way by road or by bike, in place of the walking route it showed.
- **Directions from where you are**: leave the route on a trip's walk, ride or drive, and the way is found again from where you are, with the next turn at the foot of the map.
- **Stays on the expressway**: a drive under way goes by which way you're heading, so it carries on along the KPE or MCE rather than starting from a street above the tunnel.

### Glance
- **Fits on a narrow screen**: with the Edge Tray out, the weather no longer runs off the side, "Tiba" stays on one line, and the note on what the times mean has a line of its own when there's no room for it.
