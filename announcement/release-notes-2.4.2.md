The Android app catches up with the website: a trip's timeline with your dot moving along it, Bus and train planning with no connection, Your trips in full, and a button to tilt and turn the map.

## Download

| Device | File |
|---|---|
| Android 7.0 or newer | `sg-bus-timings-2.4.2.apk` |
| Wear OS 3 or newer watch | `sg-bus-timings-2.4.2-watch.apk` |

This update is for the Android app. The desktop apps and the command line stay on 2.4.0, and any files for them on this release are that same 2.4.0.

## Updating

**Android**: tap **Update** on the bar at the top of the app, or the download-arrow button in the Edge Tray. New to the app? Download the `.apk` on your phone and open it.

**Wear OS**: nothing has changed on the watch, so there's no hurry to install 2.4.2 on it.

Your favourites and settings stay when you update.

## What's new

### On a trip
- **The timeline**: tap the trip's bar for every stop on the way, with a blue dot gliding along the line as you move.
- **The Get Off Alert buzzes OFF**: the alert buzzes "OFF" in Morse code on a trip, with the app open or not.
- **Start one from a bus's route**: tap **Get Off Alert** on a route, then the stop you're getting off at.
- **A last bus lost**: the trip looks for another way from where your last ride ends and says what to do first. It switches by itself with **Undo**, or offers **Navigate from here** or **See all ways** when there's none.
- **A faster way**: on a ride it looks from your next stop, using your first bus's live timing, counts walking or cycling the rest, and says why it's quicker.
- **Also on the trip**: **Back to Navigate**, the other buses that go straight there, which way round on the Circle Line, and minutes left.

### Navigate
- **Bus and train with no connection**: it plans on your phone when you're offline, and plans again when you reconnect. Open the app once with a connection first, so it can keep what it needs.
- **Shuttle buses and the Causeway**: a shuttle says what it costs, who may ride it and when to board, and a way across the Causeway has its immigration steps.
- **Google Maps links**: paste one into From, To or a place, and it's read and picked straight away.
- **Share**: your ETA, these directions, the start or the end.
- **Bus only**: pick a stop near you or near a saved place, with those that have a bus straight there listed first.

### The map
- **A button to tilt and turn**: hold the button with the four arrows and drag it. Up and down tilt the map, and left and right turn it. It takes the place of the 3D button, and no longer slides the map.
- **Two fingers while it follows you**: a slow two-finger drag now pans the map and stops it following you.
- **Pinching keeps following**: zooming with two fingers no longer stops the map following you.
- **The saved map**: it says when OpenFreeMap has a newer map, and **Cancel** stops a download straight away.

### Your trips and Settings
- **Your trips in full**: trips by the week, each with how long you waited and rode, **Plan again**, **Share** and **Delete**, then Trip Modes and **Clear Your trips**.
- **Find a setting**: a box at the top of Settings finds any setting by name or by what it does.
- **Language, at once**: a new language shows straight away on every screen open, and the list has its flags.
- **Choose your birthday**: now a proper button.

### The Edge Tray and SimplyGo
- **The gold uwuCoin and SimplyGo's logo**: back on their buttons.
- **Names beside the icons**: on a tablet, each button says what it is.
- **SimplyGo opens on its own**: as if from its own icon, not under SG Bus Timings, so its NFC can work properly. MyICA too.

### Report a bug
- **Screenshot a page**: go to the page you mean, anywhere in the app, and tap the floating button. It takes all of the page, top to bottom, and brings you back with what you've typed kept.
