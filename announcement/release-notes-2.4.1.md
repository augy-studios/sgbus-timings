The Android app's map, made better to use: it keeps you in the middle of the screen, its compass button works, two fingers move it, and it shows your speed when you cycle or drive.

## Download

| Device | File |
|---|---|
| Android 7.0 or newer | `sg-bus-timings-2.4.1.apk` |
| Wear OS 3 or newer watch | `sg-bus-timings-2.4.1-watch.apk` |

This update is for the Android app. The desktop apps and the command line stay on 2.4.0, and any files for them on this release are that same 2.4.0.

## Updating

**Android**: tap **Update** on the bar at the top of the app, or the download-arrow button in the Edge Tray. New to the app? Download the `.apk` on your phone and open it.

**Wear OS**: nothing has changed on the watch, so there's no hurry to install 2.4.1 on it.

Your favourites and settings stay when you update.

## What's new

### The map, on Explorer and the live map
- **It follows you properly**: while the map is following you, your dot stays in the middle of the screen as you move. It used to wait until you'd moved well off-centre.
- **The compass button stays on**: **Turn the map with the way you face** no longer switches itself off and snaps back to north.
- **Two fingers move the map**: a two-finger drag now pans in any direction. It used to stop as soon as your fingers drifted up or down.
- **A button to slide the map**: hold the button with the four arrows and drag it, and the map slides that way until you let go. The further you drag, the faster it goes.
- **A button for 3D**: tap the **3D** button to tilt the map and stand the buildings up when you're zoomed in, and tap it again to flatten it. This takes the place of dragging two fingers up or down.
- **Your speed**: **Cycle it** and **Drive it** show how fast you're going, in km/h.
