Follow a shared trip in Telegram, and the Telegram bot's new name, @sgbus_app.

## Download

| Device | File |
|---|---|
| Android 7.0 or newer | `sg-bus-timings-2.4.10.apk` |
| Wear OS 3 or newer watch | `sg-bus-timings-2.4.10-watch.apk` |

This update is for the Android app. The desktop apps and the command line stay on 2.4.8: the desktop apps already have Follow in Telegram from the website.

## Updating

**Android**: tap **Update** on the bar at the top of the app, or the download-arrow button in the Edge Tray. New to the app? Download the `.apk` on your phone and open it.

**Wear OS**: nothing has changed on the watch, so there's no hurry to install 2.4.10 on it.

Your favourites and settings stay when you update.

## What's new

### Android app
- **Follow in Telegram**: a trip someone shares live has **Follow in Telegram** under it, as on the website. It opens their trip in the Telegram bot as a live card that updates by itself until they arrive.
- **The new bot**: the Edge Tray's **Telegram bot**, **Sync with the Telegram bot** and the terminal's `help` go to [@sgbus_app](https://t.me/sgbus_app). Syncing with the bot from the app works again.

### Telegram bot
- **Live trips**: under a way in `/nav`, **Start trip** gives you a live card of when you'll arrive, a Get Off Alert before your stop, and **Share this trip** for others to follow along in their own chat with the bot.
