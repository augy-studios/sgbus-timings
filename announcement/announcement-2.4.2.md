✨UPDATE✨ SG Bus Timings 2.4.2
sgbus.uwuapps.org

Android app:
- The trip's timeline: every stop on the way, with your dot moving along it
- The Get Off Alert buzzes OFF in Morse code, and starts from a bus's route
- Last bus gone or a faster way? The trip finds it and says why
- Navigate plans with no connection, and reads pasted Google Maps links
- Shuttle buses and the Causeway, with their costs and immigration steps
- Your trips by the week, with your waits and rides, Plan again and Share
- Find a setting: search Settings by name or by what it does
- A button to tilt and turn the map
- Report a bug with a screenshot of the whole page

Download the apps and view patch notes: github.com/augy-studios/sgbus-timings/releases/latest
