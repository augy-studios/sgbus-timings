# Summary

The sidebar, and the previous and next links at the foot of every page,
follow this list. Add a page here or the build stops and says so.

Basics are what most people use: finding a stop or a bus, reading its timings,
the stops near you, and saving and setting things up. Anything that takes more
than about three taps to reach goes under Advanced.
