---
title: More from a stop
description: Narrow a stop's timings to one bus, follow that bus on from where you are, and get directions to the stop.
---

Once a stop's [timings](timings.md) are open, the buttons under the message do
more than refresh and save.

## Pick one bus: Select Bus Number

**Select Bus Number** swaps the buttons for a grid of every bus that calls at
the stop, four across and five rows deep, with favourites starred and pinned. It
is built from the bus routes rather than from what is running, so a bus that is
not running right now is still there to pick.

Tap one and the message shows only that bus. **Select Bus Number** stays, to
pick a different one, and **Back to timings** leaves the stop as it was.

## Widen and narrow

When the timings show only one bus:

- **All services** widens them out to every bus at the stop.
- The same button then reads **Collapse view**, and narrows back to the one bus.

Refreshing or favouriting keeps whichever of the two you are looking at.

## View route from here

Timings narrowed to one bus have a **View route from here** button. It lists,
as buttons, every stop that bus still calls at, from the one you are at through
to the end of its route, under a heading such as
"27 stops to go, ending at Changi Village Ter (99009)". Above the buttons are
where the bus runs and the landmarks still ahead of you.

Tap any stop to see its timings for the same bus, so you can walk the route a
stop at a time. **Back to timings** returns to where you started. At the last
stop of a route there is nothing ahead, and the button says so.

## Navigate to the stop

**Navigate**, on the last row, opens into two links, **Google Maps** and
**Citymapper**, each with walking directions to the stop. Refreshing folds them
back into the one button.
