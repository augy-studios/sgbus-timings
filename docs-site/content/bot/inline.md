---
title: Inline mode
description: Look up a stop's timings from any Telegram chat, and post them into the conversation.
---

Inline mode lets you use the bot from inside any chat, without switching to it.
It is handy for telling a friend when the bus is coming.

## Use it

1. In any chat, type `@UwUsgbus_bot` followed by a space.
2. Then type what you would search for:

   ```text
   @UwUsgbus_bot 84009
   @UwUsgbus_bot bedok
   ```

3. Results appear above the message box. Tap one to post that stop's live
   timings into the chat.

With nothing after the username, the results are your favourite stops.

## What gets posted

The posted message has the stop's timings, a **Refresh** button and a
**Directions** button. Anyone in the chat can tap **Refresh** to update it. It has
no favourite button, because whoever taps it may not be you.

> [!NOTE]
> Inline mode searches by stop code, name and road. To browse a bus's route or
> plan a journey, open the bot's own chat.
