The desktop app's window has its own title bar along the top, in your brand
colour, and the app's pages below it, laid out for the size of the window. It
opens maximised; make it smaller and it opens that size, in the same place, next
time.

## The title bar

From left to right:

- **Back** and **Forward**, through what you've opened in the app. Back closes the
  stop or bus in front first, then goes back a page.
- **Reload**, which refreshes what's in front, as pulling down does on a phone.
- The **command bar** in the middle, showing where you are. Click it to search:
  see [The command bar](#the-command-bar).
- **Copy link**, the link to what's on show, to paste anywhere. It opens the same
  stop, bus or page on the website, in the Android app, or here.
- **Screenshot** and **Long screenshot**: see [Screenshots](#screenshots).
- **Restart to update**, a green button that's only there once a new version has
  downloaded: see [Updates](install.md#updates).
- On Windows and Linux, **Minimise**, **Maximise** and **Close**. Closing the
  window keeps the app running in the [tray](tray-and-widgets.md#the-tray). On a
  Mac, the window's own three buttons sit at the bar's left.

Drag the bar to move the window, and double-click it to maximise it or make it
smaller again. Hover over any button to see what it does, and its shortcut where
it has one.

The app opens only once: opening it again, from the Start menu, the Dock or a
link, brings its window to the front.

## The command bar

**Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac), or a click on the middle of the title bar, opens the
command bar. Type to find:

- **Buses**: type a number, such as `174`, and pick it for its route.
- **Bus stops**: a name, road or 5-digit code, as in [Bus timings](../android/search.md).
- **Commands**: your pages, Settings, Theme, and everything the title bar and the
  tray do, such as **Bus stops near me**, **Copy link**, **Take a long screenshot**,
  **Full screen**, **Add a Bus timings widget**, **Make it yours…** and **Check
  for updates…**. With nothing typed, it lists them all.

Move through the list with the arrow keys and press **Enter**, or click one. **Esc**
closes it.

You can also paste a link into it:

- A link to the website, such as `https://sgbus.uwuapps.org/#84009`, or a
  `uwu://sgbus/` link, opens that stop, bus or page in the app.
- A `uwu://` link for another UwU app, such as uwuPromptr, opens that app, if
  you have it.
- Any other website opens in your browser.

**Ctrl+L** or **F6** (**⌘L** on a Mac) opens the command bar with the link to
what's on show already in it, to copy or change, as a browser's address bar
does.

## The navigation rail and split panes

The app fits itself to the window's width:

- **Narrower than 720 pixels**: the same layout as the phone, with the
  [Edge Tray](../android/main-screen.md#the-edge-tray) down the side.
- **From 720 pixels**: a **navigation rail** down the left, with your pages' buttons,
  takes the Edge Tray's place.
- **Wider than 1024 pixels**: the page's list sits in a column on the left, and a
  stop, a bus or a link you open shows **beside it**, so you can go down your
  favourites or search results and see each one's timings without losing your
  place. Until you pick one, the space says how. The departures board still takes
  the whole window.

## Right-click menus

Right-click a stop, a bus or a favourite, in a list or a search, for:

- **Open**, or **Open in split pane** on a wide window.
- **Pin to desktop widget**: a [widget](tray-and-widgets.md#bus-timings-widgets) for
  that stop on your desktop (stops only).
- **Set as tray countdown**: its next buses [in the tray](tray-and-widgets.md#a-stops-buses-in-the-tray)
  (stops only).
- **Star** or **Unstar**, to add it to your favourites or take it away.
- **Copy link**.

In a picker, such as Navigate's **From**, **Open** picks that stop.

## Dialogs and panels

What slides up from the bottom on a phone opens here in the middle of the window
as a **dialog**, such as a picker, a menu or a form, or from the right as a
**panel**, for a list or details. **Esc**, **Ctrl+W** (**⌘W** on a Mac), its close
button or a click outside it closes it.

## Full screen

**F11** (**⌃⌘F** on a Mac) puts the app full screen, without the title bar or the
rail; press it again or **Esc** to come back. A stop's **departures board** and the
**flag-down** screen go full screen by themselves, and back when you close them.

## Screenshots

The title bar's two camera buttons take a picture of the app:

- **Screenshot**: the app as it is on screen, without the title bar.
- **Long screenshot**: the whole page, top to bottom, scrolled through for you,
  such as a stop with all its buses or the whole of a journey in Navigate.

Either is copied, ready to paste, and saved in your **Downloads** folder as
`SG Bus Timings` with the date and time (and `(long)` for a long one). A
notification says so; click it to see the file. They're also in the command bar
and, on a Mac, in the **File** menu.

## The Mac's menu bar

On a Mac, the menu bar along the top of the screen has every command, with its
shortcut:

- **SG Bus Timings**: About, **Settings…**, **Make it yours…**, **Check for
  updates…** (or **Restart to update** once one has downloaded), Hide, and
  **Quit**.
- **File**: **Add a Bus timings widget**, **Take a screenshot**, **Take a long
  screenshot** and **Close Window**.
- **Edit**: Undo, Redo, Cut, Copy, Paste, Select All, **Copy link** and **Find…**,
  which opens the command bar.
- **View**: **Reload**, **Back**, **Forward**, **Search Stops and Buses…**,
  **Open Address…**, each of your pages, and **Full screen**.
- **Window**: Minimise, Zoom and Bring All to Front.
- **Help**: **How to use SG Bus Timings**, which opens this guide, and **What's new**,
  the releases on GitHub.
