A bus's route shows every stop it calls at, in order, so you can check it goes where
you think it goes, and pick the stop to wait at.

## Open a route

- Type a bus number, such as `22` or `NR7`, into the [search box](search.md) and tap
  it.
- Or tap the route button on a bus's card in a [stop's timings](timings.md#each-buss-buttons).

The route opens on a screen of its own, with **Share** at the top.

- **Route**: the stops it starts and ends at, or, for a loop, where it starts, ends and
  turns around.
- **Via**: landmarks along the way, such as interchanges and stations.
- **Operator** and **Category**.
- **Your favourite stops** on the route first, then **All stops**, in the order the bus
  calls at them.

## Directions

Most buses run both ways. The two bars above the stops switch between them. Opened from
a stop, the direction that stop is on is picked for you. A loop has just the one.

## From this stop

Opened from a stop, **Whole route** and **From** your stop are there too. **From** shows
only the stops still ahead, numbered **Here**, **+1**, **+2** and on, and says how many
stops there are to go. It's the same list as
[Route from here](timings.md#pick-a-bus) on a stop's timings.

## Open a stop on the route

Tap any stop for its timings, narrowed to this bus. **Back to bus 22** (with your bus)
returns to the route. A stop that needs a pass or a passport says so in the list.

## Alert me to get off

On the bus? Tap **Get Off Alert**, then the stop you're getting off at. The app alerts
you two stops before it, even with the screen off. See
[Get Off Alert](get-off-alert.md).

## Save the bus

Tap **Save bus** to add it to your favourite buses. It's then starred and pinned in
every stop's timings, and is a chip in [Favourites](favourites.md) that opens this route.
