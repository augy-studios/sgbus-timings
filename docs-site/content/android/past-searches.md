Tap into a search box and, until you type, it shows what you've searched for before, newest first:
the last **24 hours**, then **Earlier**. Tap one to use it again.

## Where they show

Every search box shares one list, and each shows what it can use:

| Box | Shows |
|---|---|
| [Bus timings](search.md) | Stops, buses and postal codes |
| [Navigate](nav.md)'s **From**, **To** and **Add a stop** | Places, stations and stops |
| [Places](places.md)' **Where** | Places, stations and stops |

So a stop you searched for on Bus timings is there in Navigate's **To** next time.

## The tabs

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| Clock | Your past searches |
| Star | Your saved [places](places.md), with **Edit places** to change them. On Bus timings, your favourite stops and buses instead. |
| Calendar | In Navigate only: your [calendar](calendar.md)'s events with a location |

## A search's menu

Tap the three dots beside a search:

- **Save as place** opens [Places](places.md#save-a-new-place) with it filled in, to name and save.
- **Share** makes a "How to get to" link to it. See [Sharing](sharing.md).
- **Remove** takes it off the list.

**Clear all**, at the foot of the list, empties it.

## Where they're kept

Past searches stay on your phone. They aren't sent anywhere, but [Sync](sync.md) can copy them to
another of your devices.
