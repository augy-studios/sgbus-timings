## The stop's buttons

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Refresh**, the round arrow at the top | Fetches the latest timings. |
| **Share**, at the top | Shares a link to the stop. |
| **Save** | Adds the stop to your [favourites](favourites.md). It turns into **Saved**; tap again to remove it. |
| [**Pick a bus**](#pick-a-bus) | Narrows the stop to one bus. |
| **Directions** | Opens into **Live map**, which walks you to the stop [turn by turn](live-map.md#walk-to-a-stop-or-place), and **Citymapper**, for the way there in Citymapper's app or website. |
| [**Board**](#the-departures-board) | The stop's departures board, full screen. |
| **NFC sticker** | Writes the stop onto a [sticker](sharing.md#nfc-stickers), on a phone with NFC. |

A firm shake also refreshes the timings: handy with one hand on a pole. Turn it off
with **Shake to refresh** in [Settings](settings.md#trips-and-alerts).

Along the top of each bus's card, press and hold any of them to see its name:

| Icon | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Route** | Opens [that bus's route](bus-routes.md). |
| **Star** | Saves this bus at this stop to [Favourites](favourites.md#buses-at-stops). |
| **Raised hand** | [Flag it down](#flag-a-bus-down): its number, full screen. |
| **Round arrows** | Fetches that bus's timings alone. |

A star before the bus's number means it's one of your
[favourite buses](favourites.md#favourite-buses).

## Incoming buses

**Incoming buses**, the row of boxes above the cards, is the next bus of every service,
soonest first, coloured by how full it is. Swipe it sideways for the rest. It's the quickest
way to answer "which bus comes first?", though buses might not arrive in exactly that order.

## Clock times

Each bus shows how long till it comes, such as `3 mins 20s`. To see the time it comes
instead, such as `08:35`, pick **Clock time** under **Bus times** in
[Settings](settings.md#pages-and-edge-tray). Every bus timing in the app follows it.

## First and last bus

Under each bus's three times is its first and last bus at this stop today, using the
weekday, Saturday or Sunday timetable as fits. The **bell** beside it reminds you 20
minutes before that last bus, every day it runs: see
[Last bus reminders](reminders.md#last-bus-reminders).

## Will I make it?

Under the stop's buttons, **How far's my walk?** works out roughly how long it'd take
to walk to the stop from where you are, and which buses you'd make if you set off now:
**About a 2 min walk from you. Leave now for the 2 in 2 min.** Buses that would leave
before you got there are greyed. After you first allow your location, it does this by
itself for each stop you open.

It's the straight line with a bit added for corners, at an easy pace. For the real
walk, use **Directions**.

## Pick a bus

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/android/stop-pick.png" alt="Bedok Int with a sheet slid up: Pick a bus, then a grid of every bus number that calls there, from 7 and 7A to 854e." width="412" height="892" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Every bus that calls here, running now or not.</figcaption>

**Pick a bus** slides up every bus that calls at the stop, from the bus routes rather
than what's running now, so a bus that's stopped for the night is still there. Pick
one and the screen shows only that bus.

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/android/stop-narrowed.png" alt="Bedok Int narrowed to bus 14: Show all services and Route from here, then Pick a bus, Directions, Board and NFC sticker, the row of next buses with just bus 14, and bus 14's card." width="412" height="892" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Narrowed to bus 14.</figcaption>
</figure>

- **Show all services** widens it back out to the whole stop.
- **Only** and the bus's number narrows it down again.
- **Route from here** lists the stops that bus still calls at after this one. Tap any
  of them for its timings for the same bus.

## Flag a bus down

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/android/flag.png" alt="The number 14 in huge white figures on black, with Torch and Close buttons at the bottom." width="412" height="892" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Hold it up at a dark stop, so the captain sees which bus you want.</figcaption>
</figure>

The raised hand shows a bus's number as big as your screen allows, white on black, to
hold up at a dark stop. The screen stays on at full brightness. **Torch** turns the
torch on, steady, where your phone has one, and **Close** puts it away.

## The departures board

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/android/board.png" alt="Bedok Int's departures board on black: its name and code, the time, a Close button, then each bus with where it's going and its next three times, coloured by how full they are, and the page number at the foot." width="412" height="892" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Good on a spare phone or tablet, like an interchange's screens.</figcaption>
</figure>

**Board** shows the stop's buses full screen, as an interchange's screens do: each
bus, where it's going and its next three, how full in their colour. With more than
fit, it turns a page every 8 seconds. It refreshes every 20, and the screen stays on.
**Close** puts it away.

## Notes on some stops

Under the stop's name, a few stops carry a note:

- **One place, two stops.** Some interchanges are two stops under one name: Woodlands
  Int is 46008 and 46009. A button, such as **Also Woodlands Int (46009)**, opens the
  other one.
- **Authorised personnel only.** The stops inside Changi Airport's cargo area, and
  MINDEF's on Gombak Drive, are only for people allowed in. [Navigate](nav.md) keeps
  away from them unless you set **Restricted areas** to **I have a pass** in
  [Settings](settings.md#navigate).
- **Across the border.** Woodlands Checkpoint and the stops in Johor Bahru say so:
  you'll need your passport to go on.
- **Service changes.** When buses skip the stop today or tomorrow, a note says which,
  from the [newsfeed](service-alerts.md#the-newsfeed).

## Shuttle buses

At a stop a shuttle bus calls at, **Shuttle buses** comes under the live buses, marked
**scheduled times, not live**, each route in its colour. The shuttles are NUS's and
NTU's campus buses and the island's others anyone can ride, such as IKEA's, Changi
Airport's and SGH's.

- **Every 4–6 min** while it runs at a frequency, with when it stops for the day.
- Its **next three departures** from where it starts, for a route that keeps to a
  timetable.
- **No bus till 14:16**, **First bus 09:07**, **No more buses today** or **Not running
  today**, when it isn't running now.

Where an operator shows its buses live, the row also says **Live: 4 min**. A paid one
says its fare; some say **Hotel guests only** or **Residents only**. The line under
the list says whether it's **NUS term timings** or **NUS vacation timings**. Public
holidays run to Sunday's times.

A stop only shuttles use, such as PGP or IKEA Tampines, opens the same way, with a
line saying **Only shuttle buses stop here**, and **Directions**. Tap a shuttle for
its route: what its timetable says now, and its stops in order. On
[Explorer](explorer.md#shuttle-buses), their routes and own stops are on the map.

## Coming from a list

A stop opened from a [bus route](bus-routes.md), a [journey](route-planner.md),
[Navigate](nav.md) or the stops near you remembers where you came from: a button at
the top, **Back to route**, **Back to journey**, **Back to bus 22**, **Back to nearby
stops** or **Back to Navigate**, goes back there.

## When no buses show

"No live buses right now" means LTA reports nothing on its way to this stop. That's
normal late at night and early in the morning. If it happens in the middle of the
day, see [No timings show up](../help/troubleshooting.md#no-timings-show-up). A stop's
timings need a connection: see [With no connection](updates.md#with-no-connection).
