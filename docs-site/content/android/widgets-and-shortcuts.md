## The Bus timings widget

Put a stop's live timings on your home screen:

1. Press and hold an empty part of your home screen, tap **Widgets**, and find **SG Bus Timings**.
2. Drag **Bus timings** onto the home screen.
3. Pick one of your favourite stops, or type a stop's 5-digit code. To show only some buses, type their numbers, such as `15, 62`. Tap **Save**.

Press and hold the widget to resize it any way: it shows as many buses as fit, and scrolls for the rest. The refresh button fetches the latest timings; it also refreshes
itself about every 15 minutes, and says when it last did. Tap the stop's name to pick another, a bus to open it in the app, or anywhere else on it to open the stop. Add as
many as you like, each for its own stop.

## Home and Work, a tap away

Once Home or Work is set in [Places](places.md):

- Long-press the app's icon for **Bus home** and **Bus to work**: each opens Navigate from where you are.
- Add the **Bus home** and **Bus to work** tiles to Quick Settings: pull down twice from the top of the screen, tap the pencil, and drag them in.

## The app's icon

Long-press the app's icon for shortcuts: **Near me**, **Navigate**, **Alerts** and **Card value**, or, with Home or Work set, **Bus home** and **Bus to work** in place of the
last two. Drag one onto your home screen for an icon of its own.

## SimplyGo and MyICA

The **SimplyGo** button in the Edge Tray opens the SimplyGo app itself, on its own as if from its icon, so its NFC works as usual. It opens the store page only if the app isn't
installed. A trip's **MyICA** button does the same for MyICA Mobile.

## Links that open in the app

The website's own links, shared links and places shared from Google Maps open in the app. See [Links you can share](../web/links.md). So do `uwu://sgbus/` links:

| Link | Opens |
|---|---|
| `uwu://sgbus/` | The app |
| `uwu://sgbus/84009` | That stop's timings |
| `uwu://sgbus/bus/22` | A bus's route |
| `uwu://sgbus/nav` | Navigate |
| `uwu://sgbus/near` | Stops near you |
| `uwu://sgbus/map/84009` | The live map, walking to that stop |

AI assistants on your phone or elsewhere can open the app at the right place with the links in its
[guide for AI assistants](https://sgbus.uwuapps.org/llms.txt).
