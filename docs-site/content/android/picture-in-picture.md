---
title: Picture-in-picture
description: During a trip or with the live map open, the Android app shrinks into a small window over your other apps.
---

During a [trip](get-off-alert.md), or with the [live map](live-map.md) open, going to your home screen or another app shrinks SG Bus Timings into a small window over it, showing just the map or
how far along your trip you are. Drag it where you like, tap it for its controls, or drag it off the screen to close it. Anywhere else in the app, going home closes it as usual.

## Move between parts

On a trip by bus or train with more than one part, tap the small window for **previous** and **next** buttons, as on a video. They move the trip to the part before or the next, as the buttons on the
[trip bar](get-off-alert.md#the-trip-bar) do. **Previous** is grey on the first part, and **next** on the last, so it can't end your trip.

## Turn it off

To keep it from shrinking at all, open [Settings](settings.md#trips-and-alerts), **Trips and alerts**, and set **Picture-in-picture** to **Off**. The app then goes to the background like any other,
and the [Get Off Alert](get-off-alert.md) still comes as a notification.

## If it doesn't shrink

With the setting on, picture-in-picture may be off for the app in Android: your phone's **Settings** > **Apps** > **SG Bus Timings** > **Picture-in-picture**.
