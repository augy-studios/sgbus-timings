Your calendar's events with a location, for the next two weeks, show in [Navigate](nav.md). Tap one
for the way there, arriving by its start.

## Your phone's calendar

The app can read your phone's own calendar: every calendar on the phone, Google, Outlook, Samsung and
the rest, with nothing to paste.

1. In [Navigate](nav.md), tap **From** or **To**, then the calendar tab, then **Use this phone's
   calendar**. Or in [Settings](settings.md#you), **You**, tap **Calendar settings**, then **Use this
   phone's calendar**.
2. The first time, tap **Allow calendar access**, then **Allow** on Android's prompt.
3. Your events with a location show, and are read fresh each time you open the tab.

Nothing leaves the phone except each event's location, looked up to find the place.

Refused the prompt? Tap **Allow calendar access** again. After a second refusal Android stops asking,
and the button opens the app's settings instead: turn on **Calendar** under **Permissions**.

To use a calendar that isn't on your phone, link it by its **private iCal link** in **Calendar
settings**: copy the link, paste it in, and tap **Link**.

| Calendar | Where to find the link |
|---|---|
| **Google Calendar** | On a computer: Settings, then your calendar under "Settings for my calendars", then **Integrate calendar**. Copy **Secret address in iCal format**. |
| **Outlook** | Settings, Calendar, **Shared calendars**. Under Publish a calendar, pick the calendar and "Can view all details", tap **Publish**, then copy the **ICS** link. |
| **iCloud** | In Calendar, share the calendar and turn on **Public Calendar**, then copy its link. Anyone with that link can see the calendar. |

> [!WARNING]
> Treat the link like a password: anyone who has it can see your calendar. The link is kept on your
> phone, and read through the site's server, which keeps nothing.

### Or import a file

**Or import an .ics file** picks a calendar file exported from your calendar app. It's read on your
phone only and never sent anywhere, but it's a snapshot: import it again for new events.

## Plan the way to an event

Tap Navigate's **To**, then the calendar tab. Events are listed under **Today**, then **Later**. Tap one,
and Navigate plans the way there from where you are, set to **Arrive by** the event's start. An all-day
event, or one more than a week away, plans for **Leave now** instead.

An event stays listed until it ends, so you can still plan the way to one you're late for.

A location the app can't place says **Location not found. Tap to pick it.** Tap it, and pick the place: the
app remembers it.

## An event's menu

Tap the three dots beside an event:

- **Save as place** opens [Places](places.md#save-a-new-place) with it filled in.
- **Share place** makes a "How to get to" link to it.
- **Hide this event** takes it off the list.

## Remove it

**Remove calendar**, in **Calendar settings**, stops showing your calendar and forgets the link, file or
phone calendar choice on this device.
