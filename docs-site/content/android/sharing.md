## The share card

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/android/share-card.png" alt="A share card slid up over Places: How to get to: One Raffles Place, a map, its short link, and buttons for More, Telegram, WhatsApp, Copy link and Offline Sharing." width="412" height="892" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>A share card: its map, its short link and how to send it.</figcaption>
</figure>

Sharing a place, directions, your ETA or a feat opens a card with a map and a short link. **More** opens Android's own share sheet. **Telegram** and **WhatsApp** open those apps
straight away, ready to pick a chat. **Copy link** copies it, and [**Offline Sharing**](#offline-sharing) hands it to a phone next to you.

Offline, there's no short link to send, but Offline Sharing still works.

## Share my ETA

On a trip, **Share my ETA** in [Navigate](nav.md#share-it), or the share button on the
[trip's screen](get-off-alert.md#the-timeline), gives a link to your live progress: "Sam will arrive in 12 min", around what time, which bus or train you're on and how many stops are left.
The name is the one in [Settings](settings.md#you), if you set one.

Whoever opens it sees it update by itself. It keeps going with the screen off. It ends when you arrive or end the trip, or when you tap **Stop sharing my ETA** on its card.

Open someone else's, and **Live progress** shows when they'll arrive and their stops ahead, with **Live map** for their dot moving along their trip and **Directions there** to plan your own way there.

## Offline Sharing

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/android/offline-share.png" alt="An Offline Sharing sheet for One Raffles Place: Hold the backs of your phones together, then Phones nearby with Alex, Tap to send, a note on Receive nearby, and Write it to an NFC sticker." width="412" height="892" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Hold the phones together, or pick a phone nearby.</figcaption>
</figure>

**Offline Sharing** on a share card hands a place, directions or your live ETA to a phone next to you, with no internet on either phone. Tap it, then either:

- **Hold the backs of your phones together.** Their phone needs NFC on and to be unlocked, but nothing open: it opens in SG Bus Timings if they have it, or in their browser if not. Hold
  another phone to yours to send it again.
- **Send it to a phone nearby**, without touching, over Bluetooth. On their phone, they open SG Bus Timings and tap **Receive nearby** in the Edge Tray. Their phone shows up on yours by the
  name in their [Settings](settings.md#you), or its model; tap it to send. The first time, both phones ask to use **Nearby devices**.

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/android/offline-receive.png" alt="A Receive nearby sheet: Ready to receive. On their phone: Share, then Offline Sharing, and pick Sam. Or hold the backs of the phones together: that works without this open." width="412" height="892" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Receive nearby, on the phone getting it.</figcaption>
</figure>

Directions go across with both ends and the time, so they get every way there. Planning the way, and following a live ETA, still needs the internet on their phone once it's open. Only links to
SG Bus Timings are taken.

The phone sharing needs NFC, for a tap, or Google Play services, to send without touching. On a phone with neither, the button isn't there.

## NFC stickers

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/android/sticker.png" alt="An NFC sticker sheet: any blank NFC sticker, NTAG213 or bigger, will do, and Hold a blank NFC sticker to the back of the phone." width="412" height="892" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Waiting for a sticker to write to.</figcaption>
</figure>

Write a stop, a place or a way home onto a blank NFC sticker (an NTAG213 or bigger): stick it by the door, and a tap of any phone opens it, in SG Bus Timings or the browser. A stop's
[timings](timings.md#the-stops-buttons) have **NFC sticker**; for a place or directions, use **Write it to an NFC sticker** in [Offline Sharing](#offline-sharing). Hold the sticker to the back of
the phone until it says it's written. Writing over one replaces what was on it.
