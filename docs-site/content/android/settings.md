Open **Settings** with the gear at the foot of the [Edge Tray](main-screen.md#the-edge-tray).
Tap a group to open it, and the back arrow for the groups again. Changes save as you make
them, with no Save button, except where a screen has its own.

## Find a setting

At the top of **Settings**, **Find a setting** lists the settings with every word you type,
by name or by what they do: `morse`, `tray` or `bahasa`. Tap one to go straight to it.

## The app's language

The app has a language of its own in Android. On Android 13 and later, open your phone's
**Settings**, **Apps**, **SG Bus Timings**, **Language**, and pick **English**, **Bahasa
Melayu**, **தமிழ்** (Tamil) or **中文** (Chinese).

It's the same language as **Language** in the app's Settings, whichever you change:

- Pick one in Android's settings and the app follows, at once if it's open.
- Pick one in the app, and Android's setting changes to match, so the app's notifications,
  its widget and its update page are in it too.
- **System default** in Android's settings puts the app back on your phone's language.

Changing it takes a sip from Singapore's famous language-changing cup: it spins for under a second with the tea swishing around it, and the whole app changes behind it. With animations off in Android's settings, the language just changes.

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/android/magic-cup.png" alt="A blue and white porcelain cup painted with flowers, tea swirling inside it, in the middle of the screen over the words Bahasa Melayu." width="412" height="892" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>One sip, one new language.</figcaption>
</figure>

On Android 12 and older, Android has no setting for it: the app's own **Language** sets both.

The app's text also follows your phone's font size (your phone's **Settings**, **Display**,
**Font size**), changed while the app's away or not.

## Report a bug

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/android/bug.png" alt="Report a bug: What went wrong?, How to reach you (optional), then Pictures with Add pictures and Screenshot a page, what's sent with it, and Send." width="412" height="892" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Report a bug, with Screenshot a page.</figcaption>
</figure>

In **Help and extras**, **Report a bug** sends a note to Augy.

1. Under **What went wrong?**, say what you did, what happened, and what you expected.
2. Under **How to reach you**, add your Telegram @name or your email if you'd like an
   answer. It's optional.
3. Add up to three pictures. **Add pictures** picks them from your phone. **Screenshot a
   page** puts the report away so you can go to the page you mean, anywhere in the app,
   with a floating **Screenshot this page** button. That takes the whole page, top to
   bottom, and brings you back. The button isn't in the picture, and what you've typed is
   still there. A map may come out blank.
4. Read **Sent with it, to help find the bug**: the app's version, your phone, the page on
   show and your language go with your words.
5. Tap **Send**. It reaches Augy on Telegram, and the app says **Sent as report
   20261103-7K3Q**. Mention that ID if you write to Augy about it.

Offline, what you've typed stays here to send once you're back online. Or use **Email it
instead**.
