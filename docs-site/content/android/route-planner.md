---
title: Navigate by bus only
description: Navigate's Bus only tab in the Android app: every bus between two stops, or the quickest journeys with a change, with live timings.
---

Navigate's **Bus only** tab answers "which bus gets me from here to there?" Give it two stops,
and it lists every bus that runs from one to the other. When no single bus does, it finds the
quickest journeys with up to three changes. It works it all out on your phone. For trains as
well, and any place rather than a stop, use [Bus and train](nav.md).

## Set the two ends

1. Tap **Navigate**, the arrowhead in the Edge Tray, then the **Bus only** tab.
2. Tap **From**. In the sheet, type a stop's name or code, or:
   - tap **Pick a start stop near me**, for the stops around you;
   - tap **Home**, **Work** or another of your [places](places.md), for the stops near it, or
     the stop itself if the place is one;
   - or tap one of your favourite stops, listed there.
3. Do the same in **To**.

The swap button between the two ends turns the route around, and the star beside it, once both
are set, saves it to [Favourites](favourites.md).

### Direct buses to the other end

Once one end is set, the stops offered for the other do some of the planning for you. Those with a
bus straight to your end, or straight from your start, go to the top with their buses shown, and
a line says how many there are. When none has one, it says so, and the planner looks for journeys
with a change.

## Pick a time

**Leave now** is picked to start with. **Leave at** and **Arrive by** plan for another time: pick
the **Day**, today or any of the next six, and the time.

Only buses running at that time are offered, with public holidays counted as Sundays. A bus that
runs the whole way but isn't running then is named under the list, as **Not running at that
time**.

## Buses that run the whole way

If one or more buses run from one stop to the other without a change, they're listed, with your
favourite buses starred. Only a bus heading the right way counts. Tap one for the start stop's
[timings](timings.md) for it; **Back to route** returns here.

## Journeys with a change

When no single bus links the two stops, you get the quickest journeys instead. Each shows its buses
in order, roughly how long it takes on the move, how many changes, where to change, and where you
get off when that isn't your end stop.

A journey can take up to three changes, and a walk of up to 200 m: to a stop across the road at
either end, or between two buses. At an interchange, terminal or station, a change can be between
any two of its stops up to 400 m apart.

### Wrong side of the road?

If the quickest buses leave from the stop across the road, it says so, with how far away it is, and
a button to **Use** that stop instead.

## Open a journey

Tap a journey for it leg by leg, with live timings: the buses due before you'd get to a stop struck
through as **Gone by the time you get there**, and **The one you'd catch** picked out. Each leg has
a button for that stop's timings for the bus. **Start trip** starts a
[Get Off Alert](get-off-alert.md) on it, and **Back to route** goes back.

Live timings show from half an hour before you set off. Times are rough estimates from distance,
counting an average wait for each bus.

## Across the Causeway

A journey across the Causeway counts time to clear immigration at both checkpoints, as a step of its
own. Set your own in [Settings](settings.md#navigate), **Immigration time**.
