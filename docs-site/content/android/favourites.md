title: Favourites
description: Save the stops, buses and routes you use in the Android app, group them into lists, and open any of them with one tap.
---

**Favourites**, the star in the Edge Tray, has everything you've saved, in up to five
groups: **Bus stops**, **Buses**, **Buses at stops**, **Bus-only routes** and **Navs**.
The number on the star is how many stops you have.

## Save a favourite

| To save | Do this | The chip opens |
|---|---|---|
| A bus stop | Tap **Save** on the stop's timings | That stop's timings |
| A bus | Tap **Save bus** on the bus's [route](bus-routes.md) | That bus's route |
| A bus at one stop | Tap the star on the bus's card in the stop's timings | That stop's timings, for just your buses there |
| A bus-only route | Tap the star between the two ends on Navigate's [Bus only](route-planner.md) tab | Bus only, with both ends set |
| A nav | Tap the star at the top of [Navigate](nav.md) | Navigate, with both ends set, and its stop on the way if it has one |

Tap the button or star again to remove the favourite.

## Favourite buses

A favourite bus is starred wherever it shows up, and pinned in every stop's timings, to
the top of the list by default, so the buses you actually catch are the first thing you
read. On [Glance](main-screen.md#glance), your stops show only your favourite buses once
you have any.

Favourite stops are pinned in the same way in lists of stops, such as on a bus's route.

## Buses at stops

A favourite bus is that bus everywhere it goes. To save a bus only at the stop where you
catch it, tap the star on its card in that stop's timings. **Buses at stops** then has a
chip for that stop, such as **84031 Bedok Stn Exit B · 60**. Tap it for that stop's
timings with only those buses; **Show all services** brings back the rest. The cross
removes every bus saved at that stop.

## Lists

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/android/favs-list.png" alt="Favourites on a list called Work: Choose what's in it, Rename and Delete list, then one stop and one bus." width="412" height="892" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>A list, with its own buttons.</figcaption>
</figure>

The tabs at the top are your lists. **Favourites** comes first: it has every favourite,
and can't be deleted or renamed. To group some of them, such as the stops and buses you
take to work:

1. Tap **New list**, give it a name, and tap **Done**.
2. Tick the stops, buses and buses at stops to put in it, then tap **Done**.
3. Tap a list's tab to see just what's in it.

On a list's tab, **Choose what's in it**, **Rename** and **Delete list** do as they say.
Deleting a list, or tapping the cross on a chip in it, only takes things out of that list:
they stay in Favourites. Removing a favourite from Favourites takes it out of every list.

Lists hold stops, buses and buses at stops. Bus-only routes and navs stay in Favourites.

A list with a stop in it is also a choice on [Glance](main-screen.md#pick-whose-buses).
The two stay in step: the list you pick in one is the one the other opens on.

## Top or bottom

In [Settings](settings.md#pages-and-edge-tray), **Pin favourite buses in timings** and
**Pin favourite stops in lists** each pick **Top** or **Bottom**.

## Where favourites are kept

Favourites are kept on your phone, in the app. To copy them to another device, or
between the app and the website, use [Sync](sync.md).
