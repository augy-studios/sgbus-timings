Every trip you take with the [Get Off Alert](get-off-alert.md) earns **uwuCoins**, and the coins buy spins of a wheel. Every spin
wins something.

## Earn them

A trip earns coins once you've arrived, if the app saw you really take at least one of its rides. The more there is to the trip,
the more it's worth:

- 5 for getting there
- 1 for every 2 km you ride, up to 30
- 3 for each change
- 4 for each bus operator you ride with, and 4 for each train operator
- 2 for each MRT or LRT line

Some trips are feats, worth 100 more, once each. A coin floats up as they come in, and the gold coin in the
[Edge Tray](main-screen.md#the-edge-tray) shows how many you have.

## Spin the wheel

Tap the gold coin, or open [Settings](settings.md#help-and-extras), **Help and extras**, **uwuCoins**. **Spin** costs 100 uwuCoins and
lands on one of eight slices at random: an **Easter egg** you haven't found yet, or more coins. Once you've found every egg, its slice
is coins too.

## Hide them

On the same screen:

- **Show uwuCoins** off hides them everywhere: the wheel, the Edge Tray's coin and the pop-ups. Trips and feats still earn them
  meanwhile.
- **Coin pop-ups** off stops the coin floating up after a trip, and the window for coins from past trips. The coins still come in.

**Achievements**, in [Settings](settings.md#achievements), lists the feats: what each asks, how far along you are, and **Done** once it's
done. Each is worth 100 uwuCoins, once. **Share** sends a feat, and the button at the top all of them. Rides count once your phone's location
shows you took them, so these can be fewer than the rides in [Your trips](trips.md).

Ride every MRT and LRT line for the celebration: the whole network in its lines' colours, turning slowly, with a gold trophy and confetti.

## Team run

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/android/team.png" alt="Team run: what it is, Your name in the team, Start a team run with a team name and a Start button, Or join one with its code with a Join button, and when a team run ends." width="412" height="892" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Team run, at the foot of Achievements.</figcaption>
</figure>

**Team run**, at the foot of Achievements, speedruns the feats with friends. Rides and trips any of you take count for the whole team, and
everyone gets each feat the team does, with its 100 uwuCoins.

1. Put in **Your name in the team**.
2. To start one, give it a name and tap **Start**, then **Invite to the team**. To join one, type its ten-letter code and tap **Join**, or open
   the link you were sent.

Hand a trip on by starting yours where a teammate's ended, within 30 minutes, and it counts as the one trip: **Continue from there** plans the
way. **Leave the team run** leaves it, and the feats you've had stay yours. A team run ends 14 days after its last ride.
