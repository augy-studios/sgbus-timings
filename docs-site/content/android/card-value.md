On a phone with NFC, the app reads an **EZ-Link** or **NETS FlashPay** card.

## Read a card

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/android/card-wait.png" alt="A Card value sheet slid up over Glance: Hold your card to the back of the phone, near the camera on most phones, keep it still until it's read." width="412" height="892" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Waiting for a card.</figcaption>
</figure>

1. Tap **Card value**, the card in the [Edge Tray](main-screen.md#the-edge-tray), or long-press the app's icon and pick **Card value**. You can also just hold the card to the
   phone: Android offers SG Bus Timings for it, even with the app closed, and the value opens straight away.
2. Hold the card flat against the back of the phone, near the camera on most phones. The moment the phone finds it, the app says **Reading your card…**: keep holding. It takes
   about a second. When it's done, the phone buzzes twice and the app says **Done: you can take your card away.**

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/android/card.png" alt="Card value: Done, you can take your card away, $23.45 on card 8009 1234 1234 1234, expires 30 Jun 2029, then Recent rides and top-ups with bus 14, a train ride and a top-up, a note on SimplyGo cards, and Read another." width="412" height="892" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>A card's value and recent rides.</figcaption>
</figure>

It shows the value, when the card expires, its auto top-up if it has one turned on, and **Recent rides and top-ups**: the last rides the card remembers, with each fare and when.
It warns when the value is under $3. **Read another** reads the next card. If NFC is off, **Turn on NFC** opens its setting.

## Good to know

Nothing is written to the card, and what it says stays on your phone. Bank cards, phones and watches have no stored value to read. A card on SimplyGo's account-based ticketing
keeps its value in your SimplyGo account instead, so for those the card can show an old figure: check the SimplyGo app.

If a card's value can't be read, open **Card data**, tap **Copy** and send it to us: it doesn't include your card's number.
