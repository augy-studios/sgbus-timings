The app opens on **Glance**: the next bus in big letters, and your stops' buses
under it. Everything else is a page in the **Edge Tray**, the column of round
buttons down the side.

The first time the app opens, it asks how much to start with:

- **Minimal**: just Bus timings, Favourites, Navigate and Places. The rest is
  switched off.
- **Standard**: everything but a few extras. The Edge Tray has no uwuCoins, Buy
  Augy a Coffee, Receive nearby, Telegram bot or Card value buttons.
- **Full**: everything on: Glance, Service alerts, Your trips and every Edge Tray
  button. Putting the window away without **Start** is Full.

Change any of it later in [Settings](settings.md), **Pages and Edge Tray**,
**Edge Tray buttons**.

## Glance

**Glance**, the eye at the top of the Edge Tray, is the page to read on the way
out:

- **Next bus**, in big letters: its number, how soon it comes (**Arriving** under
  a minute), where it's going, and the stop. A favourite bus comes first whenever
  one is coming.
- **Today's weather and air**, a tap away: see
  [Weather and air quality](weather.md).
- **Your stops**, each a card with one line per bus: its number, where it's going,
  and its next three times in minutes. **Arr** is under a minute away.

Once you have [favourite buses](favourites.md#favourite-buses), the cards show only
those, so a busy interchange is a line or two. Tap the big bus, a line or a stop's
name for its full [timings](timings.md). The times count down by themselves and
fetch again every 30 seconds. The round arrows beside the chip fetch them at once.

### Pick whose buses

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/android/glance-pick.png" alt="Glance with a sheet slid up from the bottom: Saved stops, ticked, then the list Work, Nearby, Home, Work and Gym." width="412" height="892" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>The chip opens a sheet: your stops, a list, nearby, or near a place.</figcaption>

The chip above the big bus picks the stops:

| Pick | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Saved stops** | Your [favourite](favourites.md) stops. |
| A [list](favourites.md#lists) of yours | The stops in the list, with just the list's buses. |
| **Nearby** | The stops nearest you, each with how long the walk is. Until the app may use your location, it offers **Use my location**. |
| **Home**, **Work** and your other [places](places.md) | The stops near that place. |

On **Nearby**, the big bus is the next one you'd make if you set off now, and it
says when to leave: **Leave in 3 min for**, or **Leave now for**.

Glance and Favourites stay in step: pick a list in one and the other opens on it.

## The Edge Tray

Each page has a button at the top of the tray, over a line:

- **Glance**: the next bus, the weather and your stops' buses
- **Bus timings**: the search box and the [stops near you](search.md#stops-near-me)
- **Favourites**
- **Navigate**, with its **Bus and train** and **Bus only** tabs
- **Service alerts**
- **Your trips**
- **Places**

Tap a button to switch to its page; each page keeps its place when you come back
to it. Under the line come the extras: **Explorer**, **uwuCoins** with your balance,
**Timings near a stop**, **Routines**, **Card value**, **Receive nearby**,
**Check for app updates**, **SimplyGo**, **Telegram bot**, **Buy Augy a Coffee** and
**Guide**, then **Settings** and **Theme** at the foot. The tray scrolls when they
don't all fit. **Favourites** shows how many stops you've starred.

**Press and hold** a button to see what it does. The tab beside the tray tucks it
away at the edge, leaving the whole width to the page. Its buttons unroll one after
another as it comes back.

Move the tray to the left, or turn off the buttons you don't use, in
[Settings](settings.md), **Pages and Edge Tray**. Each extra can be turned off, and
so can the Glance, Service alerts and Your trips pages.

On a wider screen, such as a tablet, each button says what it is beside its icon.

## Going back

Your phone's **Back** closes whatever's open: a sheet, then a screen such as a
stop's timings. On a page, it goes back to your home page. On your home page it
says **Press back again to exit**, and a second press within two seconds leaves the
app, while a trip carries on.

A stop's timings opened from a list have a button at the top that goes back to it,
such as **Back to route** or **Back to Navigate**. See
[Coming from a list](timings.md#coming-from-a-list).

## Custom home

In [Settings](settings.md), **Pages and Edge Tray**, **Custom home** picks the page
the app opens on, first in the Edge Tray: **Glance** to begin with, or any other. A
link to a stop, a bus or a journey still opens that. If your home page is turned
off, the app opens on Bus timings.

## The tours

<img src="/screenshots/android/tour.png" alt="Glance dimmed but for a spotlight on the next bus, with a yellow edge, and a card under it: Your next bus, big enough to read on the move. Tap it for all its times. 1 of 5, Skip and Next." width="412" height="892" loading="lazy">

The first time the app opens, a short tour shows the basics: a spotlight on one
thing at a time, with a line saying what it does. **Next** moves on, and **Skip**
ends it. Every other page has its own, the first time you open it, and so do
[Explorer](explorer.md) and the [sgbus terminal](terminal.md). A tour leaves out
what isn't on screen yet.

To see any of them again, open [Settings](settings.md), **Help and extras**,
**Show me around**.

## Your name and the greeting

The greeting on Glance and Bus timings follows the time of day, with your name if
you've set one, and wishes you a happy birthday on the day. Set both in
[Settings](settings.md#you).
