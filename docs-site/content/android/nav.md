**Navigate**, the arrowhead in the Edge Tray, gets you from any place to any other, by bus,
train or both, including the walk at each end.

## Bus and train, or Bus only

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/android/nav-empty.png" alt="Navigate with nothing set: tabs for Bus and train, picked, and Bus only; From, with Your location, Home and Work under it; Add a stop; the swap button; To, with the same buttons; and Leave now, Leave at and Arrive by." width="412" height="892" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Navigate, with the swap button between its two ends.</figcaption>
</figure>

The two tabs at the top are **Bus and train**, this page, and
[**Bus only**](route-planner.md), between two bus stops. The Edge Tray's arrowhead opens on
whichever you used last.

Switching tab keeps your two ends where it can: a stop stays that stop, and a place becomes the
bus stop at it, or the nearest one within 150 m. The time you picked comes too.

## Set where you're going

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/android/nav-end.png" alt="A sheet slid up over Navigate, titled From: a Where box, Use my location, then tabs for past searches, saved places and the calendar." width="412" height="892" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Tap From or To for this sheet.</figcaption>
</figure>

1. Tap **From**. In the sheet, type an address, a building, a postal code, an MRT or LRT
   station, a bus stop, GPS coordinates or a plus code, and tap the match. Or tap **Use my
   location**.
2. Do the same in **To**.

Under each end, **Your location**, **Home**, **Work** and your other
[places](places.md) set it with one tap. Swipe the row sideways for the rest. Until Home or
Work is set, its button is paler and edged, and tapping it asks where it is. The **swap
button** between the two ends turns the journey around.

Before you type, the sheet also has your [past searches](past-searches.md), your saved
places, and your [calendar](calendar.md)'s events, each on its own tab.

A bus stop is found however you'd [search](search.md) for it, the words in any order, so
`block 29 bahagia` finds Blk 29 on Jln Bahagia.

### Google Maps links, coordinates and plus codes

- **A Google Maps link**, pasted into the box, is read and picked straight away, short links
  included. Or in Google Maps, tap **Share** and pick **SG Bus Timings**: Navigate opens with
  the place in **To**.
- **GPS coordinates**, latitude then longitude: `1.35, 103.85`.
- **A plus code**, the code Google Maps shows for a place without an address: `6PH57RQ8+X2`,
  or short, `7RQ8+X2`.

### A stop on the way

**Add a stop**, between **From** and **To**, adds somewhere to stop between the two ends, with a cross to
take it off. Your time there isn't counted, so add what you'll spend there.

### Across the Causeway

Both ends are in Singapore, or at the stops in Johor Bahru its buses cross to: the Johor Bahru
checkpoint, JB Sentral and Larkin Terminal.

Crossing, everyone gets off at Woodlands to clear Singapore's immigration, rides any bus
across, clears Malaysia's, and gets on again (the other way round coming back). Each way across
counts time for that, as a step of its own: about 15 minutes when it's quiet and up to an hour
at the rush. Set your own in [Settings](settings.md#navigate), **Immigration time**.

## Pick a time

**Leave now** is picked to start with. Tap **Leave at** or **Arrive by**, then pick the
**Day**, today or any of the next six, and the time, in five-minute steps.

- **Leave at** lists each way with the time you'd set off and roughly when you'd get in, as
  **08:35 → 09:06**.
- **Arrive by** lists each way with when to set off. One you'd have had to leave on already
  says **Too late**.

Only buses and trains running at that time are used, for **Leave now** as well, with public
holidays counted as Sundays.

## The ways there

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/android/nav.png" alt="Navigate from Bedok Mall to One Raffles Place, Leave at picked for today at 08:35, 7 ways to get there, quickest first: the EW Line at 08:35 to 09:06 with no change, then bus 9 and the EW Line, each with its time, changes and walk." width="412" height="892" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Every worthwhile mix of bus and train, quickest first.</figcaption>
</figure>

Navigate lists the ways to get there, quickest first. It keeps the best journey for every mix
of bus and train, up to four vehicles, so a train then two buses is there to compare. Each way
shows:

- its trains and buses in order, each train line marked in its colour;
- roughly how long it takes, door to door, walking and waiting included;
- how many changes, and how far you walk;
- **OneMap ~42 min**, where OneMap's timetable-based planner rides the same way;
- a warning when LTA reports a disruption on one of its lines;
- **Last 38 from Bedok Int at 23:48**, when you'd catch a bus near its last of the day;
- a bus slowed by traffic right now: by how much, and which roads.

### Walking, cycling and driving

Under the ways, **Walk there** says how long walking the whole way takes, where it might
compete, **Cycle there** how long cycling takes, with **Cycling** set to **I have a bike** in
[Settings](settings.md#navigate), and **Drive there** how long driving takes. Each says when
it's **quicker than any bus or train**. **Walk it**, **Cycle it** and **Drive it** show the way
on the [live map](live-map.md).

### Shuttle buses

Ways can ride a [shuttle bus](timings.md#shuttle-buses) too. A shuttle's leg is in its route's
colour, with whose it is and what it costs. **Free only**, the default for **Shuttle buses** in
[Settings](settings.md#navigate), uses only the free ones. A hotel's or a condo's shuttle is only
used on a way that starts or ends there.

## With no connection

With no connection, **Bus and train** plans on your phone, from the bus routes, trains and
shuttles the app keeps, and says **Planned offline: times are estimates.** Walks are straight
lines, and there's no live traffic, last bus warning or train disruption. It plans again when
you reconnect.

**From**, **To** and a stop on the way find stations and bus stops with no connection, but not
addresses, and a Google Maps link needs a connection to read. Open the app once with a
connection first, so it can keep what it needs.

## Open a way

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/android/nav-way.png" alt="One way, part by part: All ways, Refresh and Start trip at the top, about 36 min with 1 change, a walk to Bedok Int with a Live map button, bus 9 with its next three live times and a button for its timings at Bedok Int, a walk to Bedok station's Exit B, then the East-West Line, 9 stops to Raffles Place, and leaving by Exit A." width="412" height="892" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>A way part by part, with Start trip for the Get Off Alert.</figcaption>
</figure>

Tap a way to see it part by part:

- **Walks**, with the distance and roughly how long. **Live map** opens the walk on the
  [live map](live-map.md), and **Citymapper** in Citymapper's app or website. A walk to or from a
  station says [which exit](#the-nearest-exit).
- **Trains**, with the line, the direction to take, how many stops, and where to get off, with
  [which door to stand at](#which-door-to-stand-at).
- **Buses**, with how many stops and where to get off, and the **live timings** of that bus at
  the stop you board it. A button opens that stop's timings for the bus, with **Back to
  Navigate** on them to come back. If traffic slows it, **See it on the map** shows
  [the bus's road](live-map.md#traffic-on-a-buss-way).

Live timings reach about half an hour ahead, so a way setting off later says when you'd board
instead. **All ways** goes back to the list, **Refresh** updates the timings, and **Start trip**
follows you along the way and buzzes two stops before each change and the end: see
[Get Off Alert](get-off-alert.md).

### The nearest exit

A walk to a station goes to the exit nearest where you start it: **Walk ~40 m to Bedok station,
Exit B**. A walk out of a station starts with which exit to leave by: **Leave Raffles Place
station by Exit A**. The live map and a trip go to that exit too.

### Which door to stand at

Under a train ride, a panel says where on the train to stand. It starts folded to one line, such
as **Changing at MacPherson for the Circle Line** or **Getting off at Serangoon**. Tap that line
to open it, and again to fold it.

Opened, each way off the platform has a row: its icon (escalator, lift or stairs), the doors that
stop nearest it, drawn as little platform doors in the line's colour with the numbers the real
ones have, and where it leads. The last line shows where the number is on that kind of door. At
some stations your next train is just across the platform, and it says so.

## Save it

Tap the **star** at the top of Navigate to add it to your [favourites](favourites.md), under
**Navs**, with its stop on the way if it has one. A nav and its reverse are two favourites, and a
saved nav doesn't keep a time.

## Share it

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/android/nav-share.png" alt="Navigate with a sheet slid up: Share these directions, Bedok Mall to One Raffles Place; Share start, Bedok Mall; and Share end, One Raffles Place." width="412" height="892" loading="lazy">

Tap **Share** at the top of Navigate, and pick what to send. See
[Sharing and NFC stickers](sharing.md).

| Pick | Sends |
|---|---|
| **Share my ETA** | On a trip: when you'll get there, and how far along you are, live |
| **Share these directions** | Both ends, the time if you picked one, and the way you have open |
| **Share start**, **Share end** | "How to get to" that place |
