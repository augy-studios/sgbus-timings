---
title: What the app asks for
description: Each permission the Android app can ask for, when it asks, and why.
---

The app asks for each permission only when you first use what needs it, and works without the ones you refuse. Change any of them in your phone's **Settings** > **Apps** > **SG Bus Timings** >
**Permissions**.

| Permission | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Location** | The first time you use Stops near me, Your location, the live map or a trip | Finding stops near you, the live map, and following your trip |
| **Location all the time** | The app from GitHub only: once, on your first trip, and when you pick stops for **Timings near a stop** | Asked so trips can keep your location in the background; trips work without it too. Timings near a stop needs it |
| **Alarms & reminders** | The app from Google Play only, when you tap **Allow** on Routines or in Settings | Routines, last bus reminders and Time to leave on time. They still come without it, perhaps late |
| **Notifications** | Your first trip, turning on Service alerts, or a reminder | Service alerts, the Get Off Alert, the "On a trip" notification while a trip runs, and the [reminders](reminders.md) |
| **Calendar** | Only if you choose **Use this phone's calendar**, or turn on **Time to leave** | Listing your events with a location in Navigate, and planning when to leave for them |
| **Physical activity** | The app from GitHub only, if you turn on **"On a bus?"** | Noticing you're riding |
| **Camera** | The first time you scan a stop's code | Reading the code on its pole. Nothing is recorded or sent |
| **Battery optimisation** | The app from GitHub only: once, on your first trip | Keeping a trip running in the background. You can say no |
| **NFC** | Not asked: Android allows it | Reading a card, opening the app when you hold a card to the phone, and Offline Sharing by tap |
| **Nearby devices** | The first time you send without touching, or tap **Receive nearby** | Offline Sharing to and from phones nearby, over Bluetooth. Before Android 12, it's location instead |
| **Install unknown apps** | The first time you tap **Update** | Installing its own updates from GitHub. Android still asks before each one |

For what's sent where, see [Privacy and your data](../help/privacy.md#the-android-app).
