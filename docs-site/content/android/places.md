**Places**, the pin in the Edge Tray, keeps Home, Work and any other place you go often, each
with a name of your own if you like: "Gym", "Mum's".

## Get there

Tap a place for the way there from where you are: [Navigate](nav.md) opens with it in **To**.

Every place is also a button under each end of Navigate, and a choice on
[Glance](main-screen.md#pick-whose-buses).

## Save a new place

1. Tap **Save a new place**.
2. Tap **Where**, then type an address, a building, a postal code, a station or a stop, or paste
   a Google Maps link, and tap the match. Or tap **Use my location**.
3. In **Name**, give it a name of your own, or leave it blank to call it what it is.
4. Tap **Save**.

A [past search](past-searches.md#a-searchs-menu) or a [calendar event](calendar.md#an-events-menu)
can be saved as a place from its menu too.

## Home and Work

**Home** and **Work** are always first. Until one is set it says **Not set yet**; tap it to set it.
You can also save where you are, which uses your phone's location and names the place after the
stop it's near, or **Pinned place**.

## Change, share or delete a place

Tap the three dots beside a place:

- **Edit** changes where it is, or its name.
- **Share** makes a "How to get to" link: whoever opens it gets Navigate with the place in **To**.
  See [Sharing](sharing.md).
- **Delete** removes it; for Home and Work, **Clear** unsets it.

> [!WARNING]
> Anyone with a place's link can see where it is, and place links don't expire. Think twice before
> sharing your home's link somewhere public.

## Where they're kept

Places are kept on your phone. [Sync](sync.md) copies them to another device.
