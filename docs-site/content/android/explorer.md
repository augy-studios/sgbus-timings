title: Explorer
description: Every bus stop and MRT and LRT station on the Android app's map, with a stop's buses or a station's doors a tap away.
---

**Explorer** is the [live map](live-map.md) with nothing to follow: every bus stop and every MRT and LRT
station round you, each a dot you can tap. Open it with the compass button in the
[Edge Tray](main-screen.md#the-edge-tray).

Stops are white dots ringed in green. Stations are dots in their line's colour; an interchange is in one
line's colour, edged in another's. The stations show from further out, the stops once you're close in.

It opens on you and follows you as you move. [Move, tilt and turn it](live-map.md#move-the-map) as on the
live map. The first time it opens, a short tour shows you round.

## A bus stop

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/android/explorer-stop.png" alt="Explorer with a stop's card open on its dot: its code, name and road, a pill for every bus there, most green and some grey, a key saying Running, Last 3 buses and Not running now, then Show bus timings and Directions buttons." width="412" height="892" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Every bus that stops here, by whether it's running now.</figcaption>
</figure>

Tap a stop, or within a finger's width of it, and its card opens from the dot: its number, its name and road,
and a pill for every bus that stops there:

| Pill | Means |
|---|---|
| Green | Running now. |
| Red | Running, but down to its last three buses of the day here. |
| Grey | Not running now: before its first bus or after its last today, or not on today at all. |

Tap a bus's pill for its [timings](timings.md) at the stop. **Show bus timings** closes the map and opens the
stop's timings; offline it isn't there, as timings need a connection.

The cross closes the card, and so does tapping the map elsewhere.

## A station

Tap a station for its card: its name and its codes, each in its line's colour, then one platform at a time.
Each says which way its trains go and the next stop, then the doors to stand at for the escalators, lifts and
stairs, and where each leads: the exit, another line, or a mall or terminal. The door number is the one on the
platform door, and the card shows where on the door to find it.

Tap the arrows at the sides, or swipe across, for the station's other platforms.

A station with no door details yet says so. Its name, codes and **Directions** are still there. If stops and
stations are close together, a tap goes to the station first.

## Shuttle buses

Shuttle buses are on Explorer too, NUS's and NTU's and the island's others
([the list](timings.md#shuttle-buses)): each route a thin line in its colour, along its roads, and each stop of
their own a white dot edged in the operator's colour. Tap one for its card: every shuttle route there, with what
its timetable says now, and the next bus live where the operator shows it. Paid ones show with **Shuttle buses**
on **Free and paid** in [Settings](settings.md#navigate). The operators' credits show while their routes are in
view.

## Directions

Every card ends with **Directions**. It closes the map and opens [Navigate](nav.md) on **Bus and train**, from
where you are to that stop or station.

## Back where you left it

Leave Explorer by a card's **Show bus timings** or **Directions**, and the next time you open it, it's back on
that stop or station with its card open, so you can carry on where you were. Close Explorer yourself, with its
back arrow, and it forgets: next time it opens on you.

## On a trip or a walk

The stops and stations drawn on [your trip](live-map.md#your-trip-on-a-map), and the stop a walk goes to, open the
same cards when you tap them.

## Layers

**Layers**, the stacked-sheets button, picks what Explorer shows. The map changes as you tap, and your choice is
kept for next time.

## Save the map for offline

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/android/map-save.png" alt="Explorer with a sheet slid up: Singapore's map, offline. Keep Singapore's map on this phone, so the live map shows the streets with no connection, on a trip or a walk. 616 tiles from OpenFreeMap, about 15 MB. Best on Wi-Fi. A Download the Singapore map button." width="412" height="892" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Save the map: how big it is, and the download button.</figcaption>
</figure>

Tap the download button under **Layers** for **Singapore's map, offline**. It says how many map tiles that is and
roughly how big; tap **Download the Singapore map**, best on Wi-Fi. Once it's saved, Explorer and the
[live map](live-map.md) show Singapore's streets with no connection, on a trip or a walk, right down to street
level with the buildings in 3D, and Explorer stays in the Edge Tray offline.

The same sheet then says it's saved, with **Remove** to delete it. When OpenFreeMap has a newer map out, **Update
map** fetches it, and the one saved keeps working offline meanwhile.

## Turn it off

Don't use Explorer? Turn it off in [Settings](settings.md#pages-and-edge-tray), under **Edge Tray buttons**, and its
button leaves the Edge Tray.
