Each [Easter egg](easter-eggs.md) you find unlocks a secret you don't have yet,
picked at random. There are three:

| Secret | What it does | Where it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Your colours** | The whole app in one colour of your own | Everywhere: the website and every app |
| **Your phone's colours** | The whole app in the colours of your phone's wallpaper | The Android app, on Android 12 and later |
| **Material You** | The whole app redrawn the way Android's own apps are | The Android app |

In a browser, or anywhere but the Android app, only Your colours can be had, so
your first egg unlocks it. In the Android app, each egg unlocks one of the
secrets that can be had there and you don't have yet, at random: all three on
Android 12 and later, and Your colours and Material You on earlier versions.
Eggs after that unlock nothing more, but most bring a colour preset.

Eggs found in a browser count in the Android app too, once you open it: if
you've found more eggs than you have secrets, a window, **A secret for your
eggs**, says what's just been unlocked.

Open the theme picker (the sun or moon button at the bottom of the Edge Tray)
and **Your colours** is under High contrast. Pick one colour, and the app
makes the whole theme from it: the background and cards tinted with it, text
dark or light on them, buttons in it, and softer text to go with them, for both
light and dark mode.

1. Drag or tap the colour wheel, and set **Brightness** with the slider. On a
   keyboard, focus the wheel: left and right turn the colour, up and down
   change how strong it is.
2. Or tap a preset.
3. Watch the preview, then tap **Use this colour**.

Every pair of colours is kept easy to read, so no colour you pick can make
text hard to see. If a button needs to be lighter or darker than the colour you
picked for its text to read, the picker says so. **Back to the usual colours**
takes it away again.

### The presets

**Classic**, the app's own green, is always there. Five eggs bring one more
each:

| Easter egg | Preset | Colour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 12, 34, 56, 78, 90 | Rising | Orange |
| 9, 87, 65, 43, 21 | Sunset | Purple |
| There and back again | Loop | Amber, the Circle Line's |
| The long way round | Long haul | Blue |
| Down the old railway | KTM | Green |

**Every line ridden** brings no preset. Instead, once you've found its egg and
ridden every line too, **Watch the celebration again** under Your colours plays
its 3D celebration whenever you like.

An egg found before Your colours is unlocked keeps its preset for when it is.

In the Android app on Android 12 and later, **Your phone's colours** in the
theme picker shows four of your wallpaper's colours. Tap **Use my phone's
colours** and the whole app takes them, the way Android's own apps do, kept
easy to read in light and dark. Change your wallpaper and the app follows the
next time you open it. **Back to the usual colours** takes them away.

## Material You

<figure>

In the Android app, **Material You** in the theme picker redraws the whole app
the way Android's own apps look:

- Your phone's own font.
- Solid cards in place of the see-through glass.
- Rounder corners, and pill-shaped buttons and search box.
- Windows that come up from the bottom of the screen on a phone.
- A ripple where you tap a button.

Tap **Use the Material You look** to turn it on, and **Back to the usual look**
to turn it off. It keeps whichever colours you have on, the brand colour, Your
colours or Your phone's colours, and High contrast too. It looks its best with
Your phone's colours.

## The shortcut to Your phone's colours

There's a way to Your phone's colours without an egg:

1. Tap the greeting at the top of the page three times, quickly.
2. Open Settings and tap its title, **Settings**, three times, quickly.

The two can be either way round, as long as they're within 30 seconds of each
other.

In the Android app on Android 12 and later, the app turns your phone's colours
on straight away. Anywhere else, a window says where it works.

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/secret-trick.png" alt="A window: A secret, unlocked. Your phone's colours. It works in the SG Bus Timings Android app, on Android 12 and later: the whole app in your phone's own colours. Open Theme, Later." width="396" height="268" loading="lazy">

Do it again to turn your phone's colours off. If the shortcut is what unlocked
them, that locks them again too. The shortcut doesn't use up an egg: your next
egg still unlocks a secret.

## Your eggs in the theme picker

Once you've found an egg, the bottom of the theme picker lists the ones you've
found.

<img src="/screenshots/web/secret-eggs-found.png" alt="Easter eggs in the theme picker: 6 of 6 Easter eggs found, then Every line ridden; 12, 34, 56, 78, 90; 9, 87, 65, 43, 21; There and back again; The long way round; and Down the old railway." width="358" height="164" loading="lazy">

Feats, eggs and secrets are kept on each device, as the rides they come from
are. They don't move with [Sync](sync.md).
