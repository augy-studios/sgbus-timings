---
title: Bus routes
description: See every stop a bus calls at, in both directions, or just the stops still ahead of you.
---

A bus's route shows every stop it calls at, in order, so you can check it goes
where you think it goes, and pick the stop to wait at.

## Open a route

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/bus-routes-open.png" alt="The search box with 22 typed in, and suggestions listing Bus 22, Bus 222, Bus 222A and Bus 222B with where each runs, then stops whose codes start with 22." width="996" height="362" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Buses come first in the suggestions, each with where it runs.</figcaption>
</figure>

- Type a bus number, such as `22` or `NR7`, into the search box and pick it.
- Or tap **Route** next to any service on a stop's timings.

The route opens over the page, titled with the bus number.

## What the route shows

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/bus-routes-shows.png" alt="Bus 22's route, showing Route: Ang Mo Kio Int to Eunos Int and back, Via: the interchanges and stations it passes, Operator: SBST and Category: TRUNK." width="720" height="191" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Where the bus runs, the landmarks it passes, and who runs it.</figcaption>
</figure>

- **Where it runs**: the stops it starts and ends at, or, for a loop service,
  where it starts, ends and turns around.
- **The operator** that runs it.
- **Landmarks** along the way: interchanges, MRT and LRT stations, and
  hospitals, so you can see roughly where it goes at a glance.
- **Every stop**, in the order the bus calls at them. Your favourite stops on
  the route are listed first, so the ones you use are easy to find on a long
  route.

## Directions

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/bus-routes-directions.png" alt="Two direction tabs, Ang Mo Kio Int to Eunos Int and Eunos Int to Ang Mo Kio Int, above the first stops of the route, numbered in order." width="720" height="236" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>A tab for each direction, above the stops in the order the bus calls at them.</figcaption>
</figure>

Most buses run both ways. The tabs above the stop list switch between the two
directions. When you opened the route from a stop, the direction that stop is
on is picked for you and marked "(current)". A loop service, which only runs
one way, has just the one.

## From this stop

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/bus-routes-from-stop.png" alt="Bus 60's route opened from Bedok Int, with the Whole route and From Bedok Int tabs above the stop list." width="720" height="760" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Opened from a stop, the route adds Whole route and From your stop.</figcaption>
</figure>

When you opened the route from a stop, a second pair of tabs appears:

- **Whole route** shows every stop.
- **From** your stop shows only the stops still ahead, from where you are to
  the end of the route. It is the same list as
  [Route from here](more-from-a-stop.md#route-from-here) on the timings card.

## Open a stop on the route

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/bus-routes-open-stop.png" alt="A stop's timings opened from bus 60's route, with a Back to bus 60 button under its name." width="994" height="154" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>A stop opened from the route, with the way back to it.</figcaption>
</figure>

Tap any stop in the list to open its timings, narrowed to this bus. The
timings say how many stops the bus has left to go, and **Back to bus 22** (with
your bus) returns to the route.

## Alert me to get off

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/bus-routes-get-off.png" alt="Bus 22's route with Get Off Alert switched on, a note saying Tap the stop you're getting off at, and the stops outlined for picking." width="720" height="443" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>With Get Off Alert on, tap the stop you're getting off at.</figcaption>
</figure>

On the bus? Tap **Get Off Alert** at the top of the route, then tap the stop
you're getting off at. The site follows you along the route and buzzes two stops
before it. See [Get Off Alert](get-off-alert.md).

## Save the bus

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/bus-routes-save.png" alt="The top of Bus 22's route, with Get Off Alert, a Saved button with a filled star, and close." width="720" height="83" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Save bus turns into Saved once the bus is a favourite.</figcaption>
</figure>

Tap **Save bus** at the top of the route to add the bus to your favourite
buses. It is then starred and pinned in every stop's timings, and appears as a
chip in [Favourites](favourites.md) that opens this route.
