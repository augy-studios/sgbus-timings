---
title: More from a stop
description: Narrow a stop's timings to one bus, follow that bus on from where you are, and get directions to the stop.
---

Once a stop's [timings](timings.md) are open, the row of buttons under its
name does more than show the next buses.

## Pick a bus

Tap **Pick a bus** to see a grid of every bus that calls at the stop, with your
favourite buses starred. The grid is built from the bus routes rather than from
what is running right now, so a bus that is not running, late at night or on a
Sunday, is still there to pick.

Pick one and the card shows only that bus. Then:

- **Show all services** widens the card back out to the whole stop.
- **Only bus 174** (with your bus's number) narrows it back down again.

> [!TIP]
> Type the bus number after the stop code in the search box, `84009 174`, to
> open a stop already narrowed to one bus.

## Route from here

With the card narrowed to one bus, **Route from here** lists the stops that bus
still calls at after this one, through to the end of its route. Tap any of them
to see its timings for the same bus. It is the same list as the
[From this stop](bus-routes.md#from-this-stop) tab on a bus's route.

## Navigate to the stop

Tap **Navigate** and it opens into two buttons, **Google Maps** and
**Citymapper**. Either one opens walking directions to the stop in that app or
website.
