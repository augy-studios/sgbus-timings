The button at the right end of the row under the search box shows today across
Singapore at a glance:

> 23 - 32 °C, 72 - 94 PSI, 25 - 52 PM2.5

Each is a range, from the lowest region to the highest:

| On the button | What it is |
|---|---|
| **°C** | Today's low to high temperature, across the north, south, east, west and central regions. |
| **PSI** | The 24-hour Pollutant Standards Index, the one NEA uses for air quality advice. |
| **PM2.5** | The 1-hour PM2.5 reading, in µg/m³: fine particles right now. |

Tap the button for today's weather:

- **Right now**, in central Singapore: the temperature and the sky.
- **Today**: the low and high, and the highest chance of rain.
- **Feels like**, humidity, wind and gusts, pressure, cloud cover, rain so far,
  the strongest wind, and sunrise and sunset.
- **Next hours**: the sky, temperature and chance of rain, hour by hour.
- **Rain, next two hours**: a quarter-hour by quarter-hour chart, or "dry for the
  next two hours".
- **Air quality**: PSI and PM2.5 for each of the five regions, with NEA's band.

| PSI (24-hour) | Band |
|---|---|
| 0 to 50 | Good |
| 51 to 100 | Moderate |
| 101 to 200 | Unhealthy |
| 201 to 300 | Very unhealthy |
| Above 300 | Hazardous |

| PM2.5 (1-hour, µg/m³) | Band |
|---|---|
| 0 to 55 | Normal |
| 56 to 150 | Elevated |
| 151 to 250 | High |
| 251 and above | Very high |

The forecast is from Open-Meteo; PSI and PM2.5 are NEA's, from data.gov.sg. The
button refreshes itself when you come back to the app after ten minutes or more.
