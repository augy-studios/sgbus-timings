There are nine **feats** to do, each worth 100 [uwuCoins](uwucoins.md) once,
and nine **Easter eggs** to find, one named after each feat. The eggs come from
the uwuCoins wheel, never from the feat itself: the wheel's egg slice gives you
an egg you haven't found yet, picked at random. So any spin can find any egg,
and the feats are the quickest way to the coins for spinning.

[Achievements](uwucoins.md#achievements) in Settings shows how far along each
feat is. This page shows how to do each one.

Doing them with friends? A [team run](#team-runs) counts everyone's rides and
trips together.

## What counts as a real ride

Every feat needs rides you really take, with the
[Get Off Alert](get-off-alert.md) running and your location on. A ride counts
when:

1. The app sees your phone within 300 m of where the ride starts, and then
   within 300 m of where it ends.
2. It takes at least half as long as it's planned to, and never faster than a
   bus (70 km/h) or a train (100 km/h) goes.
3. On a bus with stops between its ends, the app sees you near at least a
   quarter of them on the way. A train goes underground, where the location
   gives out, so its timing stands in for that.

A ride skipped with **Next**, a trip ended early, or a trip with no location
never counts. The bus and line feats count each ride as you finish it, over as
many trips as you like. The three trip feats need a trip taken to the end, with
at least one real ride on it.

## Every line ridden

Ride every MRT and LRT line at least once: the North South, East West, North
East, Circle, Downtown and Thomson-East Coast Lines, and the Bukit Panjang,
Sengkang and Punggol LRT. The Changi Airport branch counts as the East West
Line, and either half of an LRT loop counts as its line.

1. Open [Navigate](nav.md) and plan a way that rides a line you haven't
   ridden yet.
2. Tap **Start trip** and ride it, with your location on.
3. Check **Achievements** for how many are left, such as **6 of 9**.

When the last line counts, the app celebrates: the whole MRT and LRT network
raised in its lines' colours, a gold trophy turning above it, and confetti.

## 12, 34, 56, 78, 90

Ride buses **12**, **34**, **56**, **78** and **90**, in any order, on any
trips. Each needs to be a real ride, so start a trip in Navigate or Bus only
that uses the bus, and ride it to its stop. Search a bus's number for
[its route](bus-routes.md), to see where it goes.

## There and back again

Go somewhere by way of a stop, and come back to where you started, all on one
trip.

1. In [Navigate](nav.md), set **From** to where you are.
2. Tap **Add a stop** and pick somewhere at least 1 km away.
3. Set **To** to the same place as From, or anywhere within 300 m of it.
4. Pick a way, tap **Start trip**, and take it to the end.

## The long way round

Take one trip of over three hours, from **Start trip** to arriving. The clock
runs the whole time the trip does, so time spent at a stop on the way counts
too: [Add a stop](nav.md) somewhere, and take your time there before going on.

## Down the old railway

Travel between two places on the old KTM railway line, which ran from Woodlands
to Tanjong Pagar until 2011. The trip has to start within 400 m of one of these
and end within 400 m of another:

| Place | Where it was on the railway |
| --- | --- |
| Woodlands Train Checkpoint (bus stop `46069`) | The border, where the trains crossed |
| Cantonment station (CC31) | By the old Tanjong Pagar station |
| King Albert Park station (DT6) | By the old Bukit Timah station |

Set **From** and **To** in Navigate to two of them, start the trip, and take
it to the end.

## First to last

Take a bus at stop `01012` (Hotel Grand Pacific, on Victoria Street), the
lowest stop code, and at stop `99189`, the highest: get on or off at each, in
any order, on any trips. A stop on the way is fine, and so are separate trips
days apart. Each counts once a ride there is shown to be real.

## 100 rides

Take 100 rides, buses and trains together, each shown to be real. Rides on trips
from before uwuCoins count too, where the location saw you board.

## Ends of the island

Get off a train at each of the island's four ends: **Tuas Link** (EW33) in the
west, **Woodlands North** (TE1) in the north, **Punggol Coast** (NE18) in the
north-east and **Changi Airport** (CG2) in the east. Any trips, in any order;
each counts once a ride that ends there is shown to be real.

## Team runs

Speedrunning every feat is easier as a team. In Settings, **Achievements**,
**Team run**:

1. One of you puts in a team name and taps **Start**.
2. Tap **Invite to the team** and send the link, or read out the ten-letter
   code. The others open the link, or put the code in under **Or join one with
   its code**, and tap **Join**. Up to eight can be in a team at once.
3. Go. Every real ride and every trip taken with the Get Off Alert to the end,
   by any of you, counts for the whole team. When the team finishes a feat,
   everyone in it gets the feat and its 100 uwuCoins, said as the team's.

A trip can be **handed on**: when a teammate's trip ends, start yours within
400 m of where they got to and within 30 minutes, and the two count as one
trip, for **The long way round**, **There and back again** and **Down the old
railway**. Achievements shows the others **Continue from there** while
there's time, which plans from where they got to. Your own two trips back to
back aren't one; the hand-off has to be to someone else.

The same checks as ever apply: only rides your phone's location shows you
took count. A feat the team did before you joined isn't yours. Leaving keeps
the feats you've had from it. A team run ends 14 days after its last ride.

## Spin for the eggs

Each feat's window has **Spin the wheel**, and the wheel is in Settings, **Help
and extras**. A spin costs 100 uwuCoins. One slice in eight is the egg: it
gives you an egg you haven't found, picked at random, with what it unlocks.
The other seven give back 20 to 150 coins.

Finding all nine takes about 72 spins on average. The coin slices pay back about
half a spin each time, so that's around 3,500 uwuCoins in all. Once every egg is
found, the egg slice gives 80 coins instead.

What each egg unlocks is in [Secret features](secret-features.md). To try it
all without the trips, see [Console commands](console.md).
