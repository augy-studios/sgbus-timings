title: Explorer
description: Every bus stop and MRT and LRT station on the live map, with a stop's buses or a station's doors a tap away.
---

**Explorer** is the [live map](live-map.md) with nothing to follow: every bus
stop and every MRT and LRT station round you, each a dot you can tap. An
interchange is a pill instead, in each of its lines' colours: green, red, purple,
orange, blue, brown and the LRT's grey, always in that order. Open it with the
map button in the Edge Tray.

The first time it opens, a short tour shows you round; **Show me around** in
[Settings](settings.md) shows it again. It opens on you, close enough in to see
the stops round about, and follows you
as you move. Drag or zoom to look further; **Recentre** comes back to you. The
stations show from further out, the stops once you're close in. The compass
button turns the map with you, as on the [live map](live-map.md#turn-the-map-with-you).

## A bus stop

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/explorer-stop.png" alt="Explorer with Bedok Int's card open under its dot: 84009, Bedok Int, Bedok Nth Dr, then a pill for every bus there, most in green and a few, like 9A, 16M and 196A, in grey, a key saying Running, Last 3 buses and Not running now, then Show bus timings and Directions buttons." width="390" height="844" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Bedok Int: every bus that stops there, by whether it's running now.</figcaption>
</figure>

Tap a stop and its card opens from the dot: its number, its name and road, and a
pill for every bus that stops there:

| Pill | Means |
|---|---|
| Green | Running now. |
| Red | Running, but down to its last three buses of the day here. |
| Grey | Not running now: before its first bus or after its last today, or not on today at all. |

Running is by each bus's first and last bus at that stop today, with its live
timings to tell when only the last few are left. Tap a bus's pill for that
bus's [timings](timings.md) at the stop, on Bus timings.

**Show bus timings**, under the key, closes the map and opens the stop's
[timings](timings.md) on Bus timings, as a search would.

## A station

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/explorer-station.png" alt="Explorer with Dhoby Ghaut's card open: its codes NS24, NE6 and CC1 in red, purple and orange, then the North-South Line platform towards Jurong East, next stop Somerset: the escalators at doors 8 and 17, the lift at door 13 and the stairs at doors 5, 11, 14 and 20, each to the exit, the North-East Line and the Circle Line, with arrows at the sides for the other platforms." width="390" height="844" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Dhoby Ghaut: one platform at a time, with the doors nearest each way off it.</figcaption>
</figure>

Tap a station for its card: its name and its codes, each in its line's colour,
then one platform at a time. Each says which way its trains go and the next
stop, then the doors to stand at for the escalators, lifts and stairs, and where
each leads: the exit, another line, or a mall or terminal. The door number is
the one on the platform door, and the card shows where on the door to find it,
as [Navigate](nav.md) does.

Tap the arrows at the sides, or swipe across, for the station's other platforms,
the way each line goes.

A station with no door details yet says so. Its name, codes and Directions are
still there.

## Campus shuttles

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/explorer-campus.png" alt="Explorer over NUS's Kent Ridge campus: the shuttle routes as thin lines in their colours, green, orange, blue, grey, purple and yellow, following the roads round the campus to University Town and Kent Ridge Terminal, the campus stops as white dots edged in orange, the public bus stops in green, and a blue dot for you." width="390" height="844" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>NUS's shuttle routes along their roads, and its campus stops edged in orange.</figcaption>

NUS's and NTU's campus shuttles are on Explorer too: each route a thin line
in its colour, along its roads, and each campus stop a white dot edged in orange
(NUS) or red (NTU). Tap one for its card: every shuttle route there, with what
its timetable says now, such as **Every 4–6 min**, the next departures from
where it starts, or when the next bus is. These are scheduled times, not live:
the universities' shuttles have no live times the app can show.

## Directions

Every card ends with **Directions**. It closes the map and opens
[Navigate](nav.md) on **Bus and train**, from where you are to that stop or
station.

## Back where you left it

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/explorer-back.png" alt="Explorer opened again on Tampines Int, though you're in Bedok: the map round Tampines Int and its card open, 75009, Tampines Int, Tampines Ctrl 1, with a pill for every bus there, mostly green, a few grey, then Show bus timings and Directions." width="390" height="844" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Back from Tampines Int's timings: Explorer opens on it again, card and all.</figcaption>
</figure>

Leave Explorer by a card's **Show bus timings** or **Directions**, and the next
time you open it, it's back on that stop or station with its card open, though
you've moved, so you can carry on where you were. Close Explorer yourself, with
its back arrow, and it forgets: next time it opens on you.

## On a trip or a walk

The stops and stations drawn on [your trip](live-map.md#your-trip-on-a-map),
and the stop a [walk](live-map.md#walk-to-a-stop-or-place) goes to, open the
same cards when you tap them.

## Save the map for offline

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/explorer-save-map.png" alt="Explorer over Orchard with the Save the map sheet open at the bottom: Singapore's map, offline. Keep Singapore's map on this phone, so the live map shows the streets with no connection, on a trip or a walk. 616 tiles from OpenFreeMap, about 24 MB. Best on Wi-Fi. A Download the Singapore map button." width="390" height="844" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Save the map: the download button, and how big it is.</figcaption>
</figure>

Tap the download button at the bottom right of Explorer for **Singapore's map,
offline**. It says how many map tiles that is and roughly how big; tap
**Download the Singapore map**, best on Wi-Fi. Once it's saved, Explorer and
the [live map](live-map.md) show Singapore's streets with no connection, on a
trip or a walk, and Explorer stays in the Edge Tray offline.

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/explorer-offline-3d.png" alt="Explorer offline over Orchard Road, tilted: the streets, the station's dot and the stops, labels such as Orchard Road Post Office, Opposite Orchard Station ION, Shaw House and Boulevard, and the buildings standing up in 3D, all from the saved map." width="400" height="820" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Offline, from the saved map: right down to street level, with the buildings in 3D.</figcaption>
</figure>

The saved map goes right down to street level. Tilt it with two fingers and the
buildings stand up in 3D, with every label, just as they do online.

The same sheet then says when it was saved and how big it is, with **Remove**
to delete it. When OpenFreeMap has a newer map out, **Update map** fetches it,
and the one saved keeps working offline meanwhile.

## Turn it off

Don't use Explorer? Turn it off in Settings, under **Edge Tray buttons**, and
its button leaves the Edge Tray. Tap it again there to bring it back.
