When you open a stop, a card shows its name, its code and its road, then every
bus service that calls there with its next three buses. If you are new to the
colours and badges, read [Reading bus timings](../getting-started/reading-timings.md)
first.

## The buttons on the card

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/timings-buttons.png" alt="Bedok Int's timings card: the stop's name, code and road with Saved and Refresh buttons, Pick a bus and Directions underneath, the incoming buses bar, and the first services with their Route buttons." width="994" height="604" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Save and Refresh at the top right, and a Route button under each service's number.</figcaption>
</figure>

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Save** | Adds the stop to your [favourites](favourites.md). It turns into **Saved**; tap again to remove it. |
| **Refresh** | Fetches the latest timings. They do not refresh by themselves. |
| **Route** | On each service's row: opens [that bus's route](bus-routes.md). |
| **Flag** | On each service's row: its number as big as your screen allows, white on black, to hold up at a dark stop so the bus captain sees which bus you want. The screen stays on; in the Android app it also goes to full brightness, and **Torch** turns the torch on, steady. **Done** closes it. |
| **Round arrows** | On each service's row, under Flag: fetches that bus's timings alone, so you needn't scroll back up to **Refresh** from the bottom of a long list. |
| **Board** | The stop's departures board, full screen, like an interchange's screens: each bus, where it's going, its next three and how full they are, a page at a time. Good on a TV or a spare monitor; `#board/` and the stop's code is its [link to bookmark](links.md). |

**Pick a bus**, **Route from here** and **Directions** are covered in
[More from a stop](more-from-a-stop.md).

On a phone, a firm shake also refreshes the timings: handy with one hand on a
pole. **Shake to refresh** in [Settings](settings.md) turns it off.

## Will I make it?

Under the stop's buttons, **How far's my walk?** works out roughly how long it'd
take to walk to the stop from where you are, and which buses you'd make if you set
off now. Buses that would leave before you got there are greyed. After the first
time you allow your location, it does this by itself for each stop you open.

It's the walk in a straight line with a bit added for corners, at an easy pace:
an estimate. For the real walk, use **Directions**.

## Incoming buses

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/timings-incoming.png" alt="The Incoming buses bar: a row of bus numbers, each under its time to arrive, soonest first, with a note that buses might not arrive in exact order." width="994" height="147" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>The next bus of every service at the stop, soonest first.</figcaption>
</figure>

The bar at the top of the card lists the next bus of every service at once,
soonest first, coloured by how full it is. It is the quickest way to answer
"which bus comes first?" at a stop served by several that go your way.

## Clock times

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/timings-clock-times.png" alt="Bedok Int's timings with Bus times on Clock time: the incoming buses bar with 08:31 over buses 33, 33B, 155 and 228, then bus 14's next three at 08:35, 08:43 and 08:52, and bus 60's at 08:43, 08:52 and 09:07, each with its badges." width="342" height="1074" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>With Clock time, each bus shows the time it comes.</figcaption>
</figure>

Each bus shows how long till it comes, such as `3 mins 20s`. To see the time it
comes instead, such as `08:35`, pick **Clock time** under **Bus times** in
[Settings](settings.md). The card, the incoming buses bar,
[Glance](main-screen.md#glance), the departures board and the next buses in
[Navigate](nav.md) all show clock times then.

## First and last bus

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/timings-first-last.png" alt="One service's row with its next three buses, and a line underneath giving today's first and last bus at this stop." width="336" height="347" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Today's first and last bus sits under each service's three buses.</figcaption>
</figure>

Under each service's three buses is its first and last bus at this stop
today, using the weekday, Saturday or Sunday timetable as fits. A service that
does not run today says so instead.

In the [Android app](android-app.md#reminders), a bell beside it sets a
reminder 20 minutes before that last bus, every day.

## Notes on some stops

Under the stop's name, a few stops carry a note:

- **One place, two stops.** Some interchanges are two stops under one name,
  each with buses of its own: Woodlands Int is 46008 and 46009. A button,
  such as **Also Woodlands Int (46009)**, opens the other one.
- **Authorised personnel only.** The stops inside Changi Airport's cargo
  area, and MINDEF's on Gombak Drive, are only for people allowed in. The
  [route planner](route-planner.md) and [Navigate](nav.md) never have you get
  on or off at one, and never ride through the cargo area, unless you work
  there: Settings' **Restricted areas**, **I have a pass**, lets them.
- **Across the border.** Woodlands Checkpoint (46101, 46109) says
  **Immigration checkpoint**, and the stops in Johor Bahru, at its checkpoint,
  JB Sentral and Larkin Terminal, say **In Malaysia**: you'll need your
  passport to go on. Search results and route lists mark them too.
- **Campus shuttles.** At a stop NUS's or NTU's campus shuttles call at, a
  **Campus shuttles** list under the buses gives each route's timetable:
  how often it runs now, or its next departures. Scheduled, not live: see
  [Campus shuttles](#campus-shuttles).

## Campus shuttles

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/timings-campus.png" alt="Ctrl Lib (16181) on Kent Ridge Crescent: its live buses 95 and 151, each with its next three, then Campus shuttles, scheduled times, not live: A1 every 4–6 min till 23:00, D1 every 6–8 min till 23:00, K at 09:04, 09:44 and 10:24 from PGP, and R1 at 08:40, 09:00 and 09:20 from Kent Vale, each in its route's colour, and NUS term timings under them." width="342" height="1344" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Ctrl Lib at NUS on a Friday morning in term: its public buses, live, then the shuttles' timetable.</figcaption>
</figure>

At a stop NUS's or NTU's campus shuttles call at, **Campus shuttles** comes
under the live buses, each route in its colour:

- **Every 4–6 min** while it runs at a frequency, with when it stops for the day.
- Its **next three departures** from where it starts, for a route that keeps
  to a timetable, such as **Leaves PGP**.
- **No bus till 14:16**, **First bus 09:07**, **No more buses today** or **Not
  running today**, with when the next one is, when it isn't running now.

These are the universities' timetables, not live times: the shuttles have none
the app can show. NUS's change in the vacation, and the line under the list
says whether it's **NUS term timings** or **NUS vacation timings**. Public
holidays run to Sunday's times. Narrowed to one bus, the list steps aside.
On [Explorer](explorer.md#campus-shuttles), the shuttles' routes and their own
stops are on the map.

## Arriving from a list

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/timings-from-list.png" alt="Bedok Nth Stn Exit A's timings, opened from bus 60's route, with a Back to bus 60 button above the stop's tools and only bus 60 listed." width="994" height="659" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Opened from bus 60's route, the stop offers Back to bus 60.</figcaption>
</figure>

When you open a stop from a [bus route](bus-routes.md), a
[planned route or journey](route-planner.md), or the list of stops near you,
the card remembers where you came from:

- A back button, **Back to route**, **Back to journey**, **Back to bus 22** or
  **Back to nearby stops**, takes you back to the list you came from.
- From a route or a journey, a note also says how many stops the bus has left,
  such as "Bus 22: 14 stops to Tampines Int".

## When no buses show

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/timings-no-buses.png" alt="Bedok Int's timings card with no services listed, only the message No live buses right now." width="996" height="258" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>What a stop looks like when LTA reports no buses on their way.</figcaption>
</figure>

"No live buses right now" means LTA reports nothing on its way to this stop.
That is normal late at night and early in the morning. If it happens in the
middle of the day, see [No timings show up](../help/troubleshooting.md#no-timings-show-up).
