---
title: Live timings
description: Everything on a stop's timings card: the next buses for every service, the incoming buses bar, and the first and last bus.
---

When you open a stop, a card shows its name, its code and its road, then every
bus service that calls there with its next three buses. If you are new to the
colours and badges, read [Reading bus timings](../getting-started/reading-timings.md)
first.

## The buttons on the card

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/timings-buttons.png" alt="Bedok Int's timings card: the stop's name, code and road with Saved and Refresh buttons, Pick a bus and Directions underneath, the incoming buses bar, and the first services with their Route buttons." width="994" height="604" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Save and Refresh at the top right, and a Route button under each service's number.</figcaption>
</figure>

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Save** | Adds the stop to your [favourites](favourites.md). It turns into **Saved**; tap again to remove it. |
| **Refresh** | Fetches the latest timings. They do not refresh by themselves. |
| **Route** | On each service's row: opens [that bus's route](bus-routes.md). |

**Pick a bus**, **Route from here** and **Directions** are covered in
[More from a stop](more-from-a-stop.md).

## Incoming buses

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/timings-incoming.png" alt="The Incoming buses bar: a row of bus numbers, each under its time to arrive, soonest first, with a note that buses might not arrive in exact order." width="994" height="147" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>The next bus of every service at the stop, soonest first.</figcaption>
</figure>

The bar at the top of the card lists the next bus of every service at once,
soonest first, coloured by how full it is. It is the quickest way to answer
"which bus comes first?" at a stop served by several that go your way.

## First and last bus

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/timings-first-last.png" alt="One service's row with its next three buses, and a line underneath giving today's first and last bus at this stop." width="336" height="347" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Today's first and last bus sits under each service's three buses.</figcaption>
</figure>

Under each service's three buses is its first and last bus at this stop
today, using the weekday, Saturday or Sunday timetable as fits. A service that
does not run today says so instead.

## Arriving from a list

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/timings-from-list.png" alt="Bedok Nth Stn Exit A's timings, opened from bus 60's route, with a Back to bus 60 button above the stop's tools and only bus 60 listed." width="994" height="659" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Opened from bus 60's route, the stop offers Back to bus 60.</figcaption>
</figure>

When you open a stop from a [bus route](bus-routes.md), a
[planned route or journey](route-planner.md), or the list of stops near you,
the card remembers where you came from:

- A back button, **Back to route**, **Back to journey**, **Back to bus 22** or
  **Back to nearby stops**, takes you back to the list you came from.
- From a route or a journey, a note also says how many stops the bus has left,
  such as "Bus 22: 14 stops to Tampines Int".

## When no buses show

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/timings-no-buses.png" alt="Bedok Int's timings card with no services listed, only the message No live buses right now." width="996" height="258" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>What a stop looks like when LTA reports no buses on their way.</figcaption>
</figure>

"No live buses right now" means LTA reports nothing on its way to this stop.
That is normal late at night and early in the morning. If it happens in the
middle of the day, see [No timings show up](../help/troubleshooting.md#no-timings-show-up).
