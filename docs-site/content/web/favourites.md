---
title: Favourites
description: Save the stops, buses and routes you use, and open any of them with one tap.
---

Tap the star at the top of the Edge Tray to open your favourites, in up to four groups:
**Bus stops**, **Buses**, **Routes** and **Navs**. The number on the star is how many
you have. Each favourite is a chip; tap it to open it, which also closes the
favourites, or tap the cross on its right to remove it.

## Save a favourite

<img src="/screenshots/web/favourites-save.png" alt="The Favourites window with four groups: bus stops Bedok Int and Tampines Int, buses 60 and 14, the route Bedok Int to Serangoon Int, and the nav Bedok Mall to Changi Airport, each a chip with a cross to remove it." width="440" height="372" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Each favourite is a chip. Tap it to open it, or its cross to remove it.</figcaption>
</figure>

| To save | Do this | The chip opens |
|---|---|---|
| A bus stop | Tap **Save** on the stop's timings | That stop's timings |
| A bus | Tap **Save bus** on the bus's [route](bus-routes.md) | That bus's route |
| A bus-only route | Tap **Save route** on Navigate's [Bus only](route-planner.md) tab | Bus only, with both ends set |
| A nav | Tap **Save nav** in [Navigate](nav.md) | Navigate, with both ends set |

A saved button reads **Saved**. Tap it again to remove the favourite.

## Favourite buses are pinned

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/favourites-pinned.png" alt="Bedok Int's timings with buses 14 and 60 starred and listed first, ahead of bus 7." width="994" height="604" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Favourite buses 14 and 60 are starred and pinned ahead of the rest.</figcaption>
</figure>

A favourite bus is starred wherever it shows up: in every stop's timings, in
the [**Pick a bus**](more-from-a-stop.md#pick-a-bus) grid, and in the route planner's list of buses. In a stop's
timings it is also pinned, to the top of the list by default, so the buses you
actually catch are the first thing you read.

Favourite stops are pinned in the same way in lists of stops, such as on a
bus's route.

## Top or bottom

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/favourites-top-or-bottom.png" alt="Settings showing Pin favourite buses in timings and Pin favourite stops in lists, each with Top and Bottom buttons, and Top chosen." width="420" height="329" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Where favourites are pinned, chosen in Settings.</figcaption>
</figure>

Open [Settings](settings.md) to choose where favourites are pinned:

- **Pin favourite buses in timings**: **Top** or **Bottom**.
- **Pin favourite stops in lists**: **Top** or **Bottom**.

## Where favourites are kept

Favourites are saved in your browser on this device only. Clearing your
browser's data for the site removes them, and another browser or device starts
with none. To copy them to another device, use
[Sync between devices](sync.md).
