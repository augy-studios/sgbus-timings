---
title: Favourites
description: Save the stops, buses and routes you use, and open any of them with one tap.
---

Favourites live in their own card under the search box, in up to three groups:
**Bus stops**, **Buses** and **Routes**. Each favourite is a chip; tap it to open
it, or tap the cross on its right to remove it.

## Save a favourite

| To save | Do this | The chip opens |
|---|---|---|
| A bus stop | Tap **Save** on the stop's timings | That stop's timings |
| A bus | Tap **Save bus** on the bus's [route](bus-routes.md) | That bus's route |
| A route | Tap **Save route** in the [route planner](route-planner.md) | The planner, with both ends set |

A saved button reads **Saved**. Tap it again to remove the favourite.

## Favourite buses are pinned

A favourite bus is starred wherever it shows up: in every stop's timings, in
the [**Pick a bus**](more-from-a-stop.md#pick-a-bus) grid, and in the route planner's list of buses. In a stop's
timings it is also pinned, to the top of the list by default, so the buses you
actually catch are the first thing you read.

Favourite stops are pinned in the same way in lists of stops, such as on a
bus's route.

## Top or bottom

Open [Settings](settings.md) to choose where favourites are pinned:

- **Pin favourite buses in timings**: **Top** or **Bottom**.
- **Pin favourite stops in lists**: **Top** or **Bottom**.

## Where favourites are kept

Favourites are saved in your browser on this device only. Clearing your
browser's data for the site removes them, and another browser or device starts
with none. To copy them to another device, use
[Sync favourites between devices](sync.md).
