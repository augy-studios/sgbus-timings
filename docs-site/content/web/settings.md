## Settings

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/settings-settings.png" alt="The Settings window: a Find a setting box, then its groups, each with what's in it: You, Pages and Edge Tray, Trips and alerts, Navigate, Help and extras (Show me around, sgbus terminal, Report a bug) and Sync." width="396" height="574" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Settings opens on its groups. Changes save as you make them.</figcaption>
</figure>

Open Settings with the gear in the Edge Tray, or by tapping the app's title.
It opens on its groups: **You**, **Pages and Edge Tray**, **Trips and alerts**,
**Navigate**, **Help and extras** and **Sync**. Tap one to open it, and the back
arrow at the top for the groups again. Or type in **Find a setting** to go
straight to one.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Language** | Opens **Choose a language**: **English**, **中文** (Chinese), **Bahasa Melayu** or **தமிழ்** (Tamil), each with a flag, and a tick on the one in use. Until you pick one, the app uses your phone's language if it's one of these, or English. Picking one opens the app again in it. Some parts, such as the live map and the share cards, are still in English. In Chinese, Malay or Tamil, **Tell us** under the list emails Augy about a translation that looks wrong. |
| **Your name, for the greeting** | The greeting at the top greets you by name: "Good Morning, Sam!" Leave it blank for no name. |
| **Birthday** | On the day, the greeting becomes "Happy 28th Birthday, Sam!", on two lines so your whole name shows. The age comes from the year you were born. **Clear** removes it. |
| **Birthday confetti** | **On** (the default) or **Off**: confetti each time you open the app on your birthday. |
| **Home, Work and your places** | **Open Places** opens [Places](places.md), where Home, Work and your other places are set. |
| **Calendar** | **Calendar settings** links your calendar: see [Calendar](calendar.md). |
| **sgbus terminal** | **Open the sgbus terminal** opens the Linux app's `sgbus>` prompt, full screen: see [The sgbus terminal](terminal.md). |
| **Report a bug** | In **Help and extras**: tell Augy what went wrong, and it reaches him on Telegram. See [Report a bug](#report-a-bug). |
| **Show me around** | A button for each page's [tour](main-screen.md#the-tours), to see it again as it shows the first time that page opens. |
| **Next bus at a glance** | **On** (the default) or **Off**: the Glance page, with the next bus in big letters and your stops' buses. Off takes it out of the Edge Tray, and the app opens on Bus timings. See [Your main screen](main-screen.md#glance). |
| **Custom home** | The page the app opens on, first in the Edge Tray: **Glance** (the default), **Bus timings**, **Favourites**, **Navigate**, **Service alerts**, **Your trips** or **Places**. See [Your main screen](main-screen.md#custom-home). |
| **Pin favourite buses in timings** | **Top** or **Bottom** of a stop's timings. |
| **Get Off Alert on a trip** | **On** (the default) or **Off**: see [Get Off Alert](get-off-alert.md). |
| **Restricted areas** | **Keep out** (the default) or **I have a pass**: for people who work inside Changi Airport's cargo area or at MINDEF's stops on Gombak Drive. With a pass, [Navigate](nav.md) and the [route planner](route-planner.md) get on and off at those stops, and ride through the cargo area, as at any other. |
| **Immigration time** | How long a journey across the Causeway counts for getting off, clearing both checkpoints and getting on again. **By the time** (the default): about 15 minutes when it's quiet, up to an hour at the rush. Or your own: **15 min**, **30 min**, **45 min** or **1 hr**. See [Navigate](nav.md#across-the-causeway). |
| **Cycling** | **No bike** (the default) or **I have a bike**. With a bike, [Navigate](nav.md#traffic-walking-and-cycling) also says how long cycling there takes, and a [trip](get-off-alert.md#a-faster-way) tells you when cycling the rest of the way is quicker. |
| **Pin favourite stops in lists** | **Top** or **Bottom** of lists of stops. |
| **Picture-in-picture** | The [Android app](android-app.md#picture-in-picture) only. **On** (the default) or **Off**: whether leaving the app on a trip or the live map shrinks it into a small window over your other apps. |
| **Edge Tray side** | **Right** (the default) or **Left**: which edge of the screen the Edge Tray, the column of buttons at the top of the screen, hangs from. Its arrow tucks it away at that edge. |
| **Edge Tray buttons** | Turn off any of **[Explorer](explorer.md)**, **SimplyGo**, **Telegram bot**, **Buy Augy a Coffee** and **Guide** for a tidier tray, and in the [Android app](android-app.md#reminders), **Timings near a stop** and **Routines**. Each is on until you turn it off; tap again to bring it back. |
| **Sync with another device** | See [Sync between devices](sync.md). |

Changes save as you make them. There is no Save button.

### Your birthday

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/settings-birthday.png" alt="The top of the app on your birthday: Happy 28th Birthday, on one line and Samantha Tan Wei Ling! on the next, with confetti falling over the page." width="390" height="300" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>On your birthday: your age, your whole name, and confetti.</figcaption>
</figure>

On your birthday, the greeting wishes you a happy one, with the birthday you're
turning, worked out from the year in **Birthday**. Confetti falls each time you
open the app that day, and again when you come back to it after ten minutes or
more away. Turn it off with **Birthday confetti** in Settings. With your device
set to reduce motion, there's no confetti.

## Report a bug

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/bug-report-settings.png" alt="Settings' Help and extras: Report a bug, its button, and a note: Something not working? Tell Augy what happened, and it reaches him on Telegram." width="366" height="135" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Report a bug, in Settings' Help and extras.</figcaption>
</figure>

Something not working? Open [Settings](#settings), **Help and extras**, and tap
**Report a bug**.

1. Under **What went wrong?**, say what you did, what happened, and what you
   expected.
2. Under **How to reach you**, add your Telegram @name or your email if you'd
   like an answer. It's optional.
3. Read the line under it, **Sent with it, to help find the bug**: that's
   everything else that goes with your words.
4. Tap **Send**. It reaches Augy on Telegram, and the window says **Sent. Thank
   you! Augy will look into it.**

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/bug-report.png" alt="The Report a bug window filled in: What went wrong? says bus 60's timings at Bedok Int showed Arr for 5 minutes, but the bus didn't come till 8 minutes later; How to reach you says @sam_tan; under them, Sent with it, to help find the bug: Web, site v128, Android 15, Chrome 141, 390×844, Bus timings, stop 84009; then Send and Cancel." width="366" height="479" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Before you send it: your words, and what goes with them.</figcaption>
</figure>

What goes with it is only what helps find the bug: which app and its version,
your phone or computer and browser as they describe themselves, the size of the
window, the page you were on (with the stop's or bus's number if one was open)
and your language. Never where you are, your places or your saved stops.

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/bug-report-sent.png" alt="The Report a bug window after Send: the boxes empty again, and Sent. Thank you! Augy will look into it." width="366" height="514" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Sent: the boxes clear, ready for the next one.</figcaption>
</figure>

- What you've typed stays while the app's open, even if you close the window,
  until it's sent.
- Offline, it says so and keeps it: send it once you're back online.
- If it can't be sent from the app, it says so and offers **Email it instead**,
  with your words filled in.

## Themes

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/settings-themes.png" alt="The Theme window with Light, Dark and Time-based modes, and seven brand colour swatches." width="440" height="446" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>The theme picker: a mode, and one of seven colours.</figcaption>
</figure>

Tap the sun or moon button at the bottom of the Edge Tray to open the theme picker.
Choices apply at once and are remembered on this device.

### Mode

- **Light** is the default, whatever your device is set to.
- **Dark** stays dark.
- **Time-based** follows your device's clock: light from 09:00, dark from 18:00.
  An app left open across either time switches by itself. The picker says which
  of the two it is in right now.

### Brand colour

Seven colours tint the page, the buttons and the highlights:

| Colour | Swatch |
|---|---|
| Classic, the default | Pale green |
| Not green 1 | Pale red |
| Not green 2 | Pale blue |
| Not green 3 | Pale yellow |
| Not green 4 | Pale pink |
| Not green 5 | Pale cyan |
| Really really light green | White |

Every colour works in both light and dark mode, and text stays readable in all
fourteen combinations.

### High contrast

**Phone's setting** (the default) follows your phone's or computer's own high
contrast or "increase contrast" setting. **On** turns it on whatever that says,
and **Off** keeps it off. High contrast makes text black or white, cards solid
rather than see-through, and their edges clear.

### A secret

There's one more thing in the theme picker, but only once you've earned it. Ride
every MRT and LRT line at least once on a trip with the
[Get Off Alert](get-off-alert.md): the North-South, East-West (its Changi Airport
branch counts), North East, Circle, Downtown and Thomson-East Coast lines, and the
Bukit Panjang, Sengkang and Punggol LRTs, on as many trips as you like. Then see
what happens. Only rides you really take count: the app has to see your
phone where the ride starts and where it ends, taking about as long as the
ride does.

> This guide has the same theme picker, at the top right of every page. Its
