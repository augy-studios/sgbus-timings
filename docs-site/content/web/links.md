The address in your browser changes as you use the app, so whatever is on
screen can be bookmarked, added to your home screen, or sent to a friend.

| Link | Opens |
|---|---|
| `sgbus.uwuapps.org/#84009` | Bus stop 84009's timings |
| `sgbus.uwuapps.org/#84009,174` | Bus stop 84009, narrowed to bus 174 |
| `sgbus.uwuapps.org/#bus/22` | Bus 22's route |
| `sgbus.uwuapps.org/#route/84009/75009` | The route planner, from stop 84009 to stop 75009 |
| `sgbus.uwuapps.org/#nav/here/1.31533,103.81554~Botanic%20Gardens` | [Navigate](nav.md), from where you are to the Botanic Gardens |
| `sgbus.uwuapps.org/go/t2d6bdtkdj` | [Navigate](nav.md#share-it) to a shared place, from where you are: an end of a nav, one of your [places](places.md), a [past search](past-searches.md) or a [calendar event](calendar.md)'s location |
| `sgbus.uwuapps.org/trip/t89e11x1zg` | Navigate with shared directions, or a trip shared from [Your trips](trips.md) |
| `sgbus.uwuapps.org/eta/ttzik7wkd6` | Someone's [live progress](nav.md#share-my-eta) on a trip |
| `sgbus.uwuapps.org/#sync/BCDFGH` | Settings, with sync code `BCDFGH` filled in |
| `sgbus.uwuapps.org/#alerts` | The [Service alerts](service-alerts.md) card, where a tapped alert notification also lands |

## Share a stop

1. Open the stop.
2. Copy the address from your browser's address bar, or use the browser's
   **Share** button.

The person you send it to sees the same stop with the live timings at the time
they open it.

> [!TIP]
> A link to your stop, added to your phone's home screen, opens straight onto
> its timings. It is a handy shortcut for the stop outside your home or office.

Navigate and Plan a route have a **Share** button of their own, which works
part filled in too. See [Navigate](nav.md#share-it) and the
[route planner](route-planner.md#share-the-route).

## Sync links

A `#sync/` link only works while the device that showed its code is still
showing it. See [Sync favourites between devices](sync.md).

## App links

On a phone with the [Android app](android-app.md), every link above opens in
the app. It also opens `uwusgbus://` links, which only work there:

| Link | Opens |
|---|---|
| `uwusgbus://` | The app |
| `uwusgbus://84009` | That stop's timings |
| `uwusgbus://bus/22` | A bus's route |
| `uwusgbus://nav` | Navigate |
| `uwusgbus://near` | Stops near you |
| `uwusgbus://go/t2d6bdtkdj` | A share link, as its `sgbus.uwuapps.org/go/...` would |

Anything after `uwusgbus://` is the same as after the `#` in the app's own
links. Plenty of apps, Telegram among them, don't let custom links like these be
tapped, so share the `sgbus.uwuapps.org` links instead: they work everywhere,
and open the app where it's installed.
