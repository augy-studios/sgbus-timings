**Navigate** gets you from any place to any other, by bus, train or both,
including the walk at each end.

## Bus and train, or bus only

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/nav-tabs.png" alt="Navigate with its two tabs at the top, Bus and train picked and Bus only beside it, then the From and To boxes with Home and Work under each, the swap button, and Leave now, Leave at and Arrive by." width="396" height="531" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Navigate's two tabs: Bus and train, and Bus only.</figcaption>
</figure>

Navigate has two tabs at the top:

- **Bus and train**, this page: from any place to any other, by every
  worthwhile mix of buses and trains.
- **Bus only**, the [route planner](route-planner.md): between two bus stops,
  every bus that runs the whole way, or the quickest journeys with a change of
  bus or a short walk.

Switching tab keeps your two ends where it can: a stop stays that stop, and a
place becomes the bus stop at it, or the nearest one within 150 m. The time you
picked comes too. The Edge Tray's arrowhead opens Navigate on whichever tab you
used last.

## Set where you're going

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/nav-set-suggest.png" alt="Navigate with Bedok Mall typed into From, and suggestions dropping down below it: Bedok Mall, its car park entrance, and Djitsun Mall Bedok." width="880" height="319" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Type a place, an address, a station or a stop, and pick it from the suggestions.</figcaption>
</figure>

1. Tap **Navigate**, the arrowhead in the Edge Tray.
2. In **From**, type an address, a building, a postal code, an MRT or LRT
   station, a bus stop, GPS coordinates or a plus code, and pick it from the
   suggestions. Or tap the pin next to the box to start from where you are.
3. Do the same in **To**.

The swap button between the two boxes turns the journey around.

### Stops by any name

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/nav-any-name.png" alt="Navigate with block 29 bahagia typed into From, and Blk 29, bus stop 52129 on Jln Bahagia, suggested under it." width="396" height="294" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Block 29 Bahagia finds Blk 29 on Jln Bahagia.</figcaption>
</figure>

A bus stop is found however you'd [search](search.md) for it: the words in any
order, across the stop's name and its road, and the long forms of LTA's short
ones, **Block** for Blk, **Jalan** for Jln, **Opposite** for Opp. So
`block 29 bahagia` finds Blk 29 on Jln Bahagia.

### Coordinates and plus codes

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/nav-plus-code.png" alt="Navigate with the plus code 6PH57RQ8+X2 typed into To, and the same plus code suggested under it." width="396" height="448" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>A plus code in To.</figcaption>
</figure>

Either end can be a point on the map:

- **GPS coordinates**, latitude then longitude: `1.35, 103.85`, or
  `1.35N 103.85E`.
- **A plus code**, the code Google Maps shows for a place without an address,
  whole (`6PH57RQ8+X2`) or short (`7RQ8+X2`).

Outside Singapore and the stops across the Causeway, it says so rather than
suggesting nothing.

### Across the Causeway

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/nav-johor.png" alt="Navigate from Changi Airport Ter 1 to Larkin Ter: seven ways, quickest first, from the Changi Airport branch, East-West and Downtown Lines then bus 170 in about 156 minutes, to 858, 911 and 170 in about 186." width="880" height="986" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Changi Airport to Larkin Terminal in Johor Bahru, every way ending on the 170.</figcaption>
</figure>

Both ends are in Singapore, or at the stops in Johor Bahru its buses cross the
Causeway to: the Johor Bahru checkpoint, JB Sentral and Larkin Terminal. A
journey across the border has a **MyICA** button on the trip bar once it's
started: see [Get Off Alert](get-off-alert.md).

Crossing, everyone gets off at Woodlands to clear Singapore's immigration, rides
any bus across to the Johor Bahru checkpoint, clears Malaysia's, and gets on again
(the other way round coming back). Each way across counts time for that, and
shows it as a step of its own: about 15 minutes when it's quiet and up to an hour
at the rush, by when you'd get there (weekday mornings into Singapore, weekday
evenings and Friday nights out to Johor Bahru, Saturday mornings out and Sunday
evenings back). If you know how long you usually take, set it in
[Settings](settings.md), **Immigration time**.

### Home and Work

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/nav-home-work.png" alt="Navigate from Bedok Mall to One Raffles Place, with Home, Work, Gym and Mum's buttons under both the From and To boxes." width="880" height="244" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>From Home to Work in two taps.</figcaption>
</figure>

Under each box, **Home** and **Work** set that end to the place saved for it, and
so does a button for each other place you've saved. Set them in
[Places](places.md); until Home or Work is set, its button is dashed, and tapping
it opens Places to set it.

Tapping into either box, before you type, also lists your
[past searches](past-searches.md), your saved places, and your
[calendar](calendar.md)'s events, each on its own tab.

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/nav-phone-header.png" alt="The top of Navigate on a phone: its title with the close button beside it at the right, and Refresh, Share and Save nav on the line below." width="364" height="105" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>On a phone, close stays beside the title, with the other buttons underneath.</figcaption>
</figure>

Navigate opens in a window over the page. Close it with the button at its top
right, or by tapping anywhere outside it. Opening a bus's timings from a way
takes you to Bus timings, where **Back to Navigate** comes back to that same
way. Starting a trip closes it too, and the Edge Tray's arrowhead brings it back
with the same two places.

### From a Google Maps link

Either box also takes a Google Maps link: paste one in, and once it's read the
place is set as that end, named as Google Maps names it. Short links
(`maps.app.goo.gl`) work as well as full ones.

With the app installed on Android, you can also share a place to it. In Google
Maps, or on a Google Maps link in another app such as Telegram, tap **Share** and
pick **SG Bus Timings**. Navigate opens with the place in **To**; tap the pin by
**From** to go there from where you are.

A link to somewhere outside Singapore, or one with no place in it, says so under
the box.

## Pick a time

Under the boxes, **Leave now** is picked to start with. Tap **Leave at** or
**Arrive by** to plan for another time instead, then pick the day (today or any of
the next six) and the time.

- **Leave at** lists each way with the time you'd set off and roughly when you'd
  get in, as **08:30 → 09:05**.
- **Arrive by** lists each way with the time you'd have to set off to get in by
  then. A way you'd have had to leave on already says **Too late**.

Only buses and trains running at that time are used, for Leave now as well: a
bus counts as running between its first and last bus at that stop, for that day
of the week, with public holidays counted as Sundays, and trains between about
5:30am (6am on Sundays and public holidays) and midnight. A bus that's stopped
for the night, or only runs on weekdays, isn't offered on a Sunday.

Navigate goes back to **Leave now** each time it's opened, including from a saved
nav.

## The ways there

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/nav-ways.png" alt="Navigate from Bedok Mall to Botanic Gardens station listing 8 ways, quickest first: trains on the EW and DT lines, buses, and mixes of the two, each with its changes, walk and time." width="906" height="800" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Every worthwhile mix of bus and train, quickest first.</figcaption>
</figure>

Navigate lists the ways to get there, quickest first. It doesn't stop at the
fastest: it keeps the best journey for every mix of bus and train, up to four
vehicles, so a train then two buses, or two trains then a bus, is there to
compare. A mix that's clearly slower than another of the same kind, and no
better in changes or walking, is left out as a detour.

A change can be a short walk to another stop or station, or, at an interchange,
terminal or station, a walk of up to 400 m across it, such as Tampines Int to
Tampines Stn/Int. Every walk is timed, so a long one only shows up when it's worth
it. Staying on a bus into the interchange your next bus leaves from is
preferred over getting off early and walking, unless walking is clearly quicker.

Each way shows:

- its trains and buses in order, each train line marked in its colour;
- roughly how long it takes, door to door, walking and waiting included;
- how many changes, and how far you walk;
- **OneMap ~42 min**, where OneMap's timetable-based planner rides the same way,
  as a second opinion on the time;
- a warning when LTA reports a disruption on one of its lines.

> [!NOTE]
> No timetables are published for trains, so train times are estimates from the
> distance between stations, with an average wait to board. OneMap's time, where
> shown, comes from timetables.

## Open a way

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/nav-open.png" alt="One way from Bedok Mall to Botanic Gardens station, part by part: the walks, each train with its line, direction and stops, and the buttons All ways, Refresh and Start trip." width="906" height="668" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>A way part by part, with Start trip for the Get Off Alert.</figcaption>
</figure>

Tap a way to see it part by part:

- **Walks**, with the distance and roughly how long. **Directions** opens the
  walk on the [live map](live-map.md), or in Citymapper.
- **Trains**, with the line, the direction to take (**towards** its last
  station), how many stops, and where to get off. For almost every MRT
  station, it also says [which door to stand at](#which-door-to-stand-at).
- **Buses**, with how many stops, where to get off, and the **live timings** of
  that bus at the stop you board it. A button opens that stop's timings for the
  bus, with **Back to Navigate** on them to come back.

### Which door to stand at

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/nav-doors.png" alt="A ride on the Changi Airport branch, with a panel under it: Getting off at Changi Airport, then an escalator to T1, T3, T4 and Jewel at car 5, door 4, an escalator to T2 at car 2, door 1, a lift to T2 at car 2, door 1, and a lift to T1, T3, T4 and Jewel at car 5, door 4. Under them, Car 1 is the front of the train." width="310" height="423" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Getting off: the doors nearest each way out, and where it leads.</figcaption>
</figure>

Under a train ride, a panel says where on the train to stand. Each way off the
platform has a row: its icon (escalator, lift or stairs), where it leads, and the
doors that stop nearest it, as **Car 3 · Door 1**. Cars are counted from the front
of the train, so car 1 is the one at the front, and doors from the front of each
car, as they're numbered on the platform.

- **Getting off**, it's the ways out of the station, with the mall or terminal
  one leads to where it's somewhere in particular: Jewel, Nex, City Square.
- **Changing lines**, it's the way to your next train instead.
- **Your next train across the platform**, it just says so.

A few stations don't have their doors in yet, and a ride ending at one shows no
panel.

With a time picked, each bus and train also says roughly when you'd board it. Live
timings only reach about half an hour ahead, so for a way setting off later than
that, each bus says when you'd board it instead, counting an average wait, and its
live timings show up from half an hour before you set off.

**All ways** goes back to the list, and **Refresh** beside it updates the way's
live bus timings.

**Refresh** at the top of the window, there once both ends are set, plans the ways
again, with any new disruptions and the time now. With a way open, it updates that
way's live timings too.

**Start trip** follows you along the way and buzzes two stops before each change
and the end: see [Get Off Alert](get-off-alert.md).

## Save it

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/nav-save.png" alt="The top of Navigate, with Refresh, Share, and a Saved button with a filled star." width="898" height="75" loading="lazy">

Tap **Save nav** to add it to your [favourites](favourites.md), under **Navs**.
Like routes, a nav and its reverse are two separate favourites.

A saved nav doesn't keep a time.

## Share it

Tap **Share** at the top of Navigate, and pick what to send:

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/nav-share-menu.png" alt="The Share menu: Share my ETA, live progress until you get there; Share these directions, Bedok Mall to One Raffles Place; Share start, Bedok Mall; and Share end, One Raffles Place." width="444" height="356" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Share my ETA is there while you're on a trip.</figcaption>
</figure>

| Pick | Sends | The link |
|---|---|---|
| **Share my ETA** | On a trip: when you'll get there, and how far along you are, live | `sgbus.uwuapps.org/eta/…` |
| **Share these directions** | Both ends, the time if you picked one, and the way you have open | `sgbus.uwuapps.org/trip/…` |
| **Share start**, **Share end** | "How to get to" that place | `sgbus.uwuapps.org/go/…` |

Each opens a card with a map and the short link, and buttons to send it with
Telegram or WhatsApp, copy it, or, on a phone, open your share sheet with
**More**. In a chat, the link shows its map and what it is. In the
[Android app](android-app.md), from version 2.1.0, Telegram and WhatsApp open
straight in the app, ready to pick a chat, without going through the browser,
and [**Offline Sharing**](android-app.md#offline-sharing) sends it to a phone
next to you with no internet, by holding the phones together.

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/nav-share-place.png" alt="The How to get to card: a map of Raffles Place with a pin, One Raffles Place, and its short link, above buttons for Telegram, WhatsApp, Copy link and More." width="444" height="454" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Share end: how to get to One Raffles Place, from wherever your friend is.</figcaption>
</figure>

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/nav-share-directions.png" alt="The Share these directions card: a map with the route from Bedok to Raffles Place drawn in purple between a green start pin and a red end pin, then Bedok Mall to One Raffles Place, ~46 min, 1 change, 211 m walk, and the short link." width="444" height="475" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Share these directions, with the way you have open drawn on the map.</figcaption>
</figure>

- A **directions** link opens Navigate with the same two places and time, and the
  same way open if it still runs.
- A **start** or **end** link opens Navigate from wherever the person opening it
  is, to that place.
- An end set to **Your location** isn't sent as where you are: in a directions
  link it becomes the location of whoever opens it, and shared on its own it's
  sent as **My location**, for meeting up.

Sharing the same place or directions again gives the same link. With nothing set
yet, **Share** sends a link to Navigate itself, and the address of the page
changes to the nav as you go, so you can also bookmark it.

### Share my ETA

On a trip, **Share my ETA**, here or on the trip bar, gives a link to your live
progress: "Sam will arrive in 12 min", around what time, which bus or train
you're on and how many stops are left, and a map with where you've got to on the
route. The name is the one in [Settings](settings.md), if you set one.

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/nav-share-eta.png" alt="The Share live progress card: the route on a map with a blue pin where you are, I will arrive in 36 min at One Raffles Place, around 06:08, the short link, the send buttons, and Stop sharing my ETA." width="444" height="516" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Your ETA, as you share it.</figcaption>
</figure>

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/nav-share-eta-view.png" alt="The Live progress window a friend sees: the map, Sam will arrive in 36 min at One Raffles Place, around 06:08, On Bus 28: 11 stops to Paya Lebar Stn Exit D, part 1 of 2, Updated just now, and a Directions there button." width="444" height="516" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>What your friend sees when they open the link. Directions there plans their own way to you.</figcaption>
</figure>

Whoever opens it sees it update by itself. The app sends your progress every half
minute while it's open. With notifications allowed for the
[Get Off Alert](get-off-alert.md), the server carries on from LTA's bus positions
and the clock while the app is in the background. The link says when it was last
updated, and that the arrival time is the last estimate if it's been a while.

It ends when you arrive or end the trip, or when you tap **Stop sharing my ETA**
on its card. The link stops working three hours after its last update.
