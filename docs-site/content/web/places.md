**Places** keeps Home, Work and any other place you go often, each with a name
of your own if you like: "Gym", "Mum's". Open it with the bookmark in the Edge
Tray.

## Get there

Tap a place for the way there from where you are: [Navigate](nav.md) opens with
it in **To**, and finds where you are for **From**.

Every place is also a button under each end of Navigate, on its Bus and train
and [Bus only](route-planner.md) tabs, after Home and Work. The buttons wrap onto
more lines once there are more than fit.

## Save a new place

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/places-new.png" alt="The Save a new place form: Jewel Changi Airport found in Where, Saving: Jewel Changi Airport under it, Airport typed as the name, and Save and Cancel buttons." width="406" height="305" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Find the place, then name it if you like.</figcaption>
</figure>

1. Tap **Save a new place**.
2. In **Where**, type an address, a building, a postal code, a station or a stop,
   or paste a Google Maps link, and pick it from the suggestions. Or tap the pin
   to use where you are now. Tapping into the box first shows your
   [past searches](past-searches.md) to pick from.
3. In **Name**, give it a name of your own, or leave it blank to call it what it
   is.
4. Tap **Save**.

A [past search](past-searches.md#a-searchs-menu) or a
[calendar event](calendar.md#an-events-menu) can be saved as a place from its
menu too.

## Home and Work

**Home** and **Work** are always first. Until one is set it says so, and its
buttons under Navigate's and the planner's ends are dashed; tapping either sets
it. Their menus edit, share or clear them.

## Change, share or delete a place

Tap the menu, the three dots, beside a place:

- **Edit** changes where it is, or its name.
- **Share** makes a "How to get to" link: whoever opens it gets Navigate with the
  place in **To**, from wherever they are. See [Links you can share](links.md).
- **Delete** removes it; for Home and Work, **Clear** unsets it.

> [!WARNING]
> Anyone with a place's link can see where it is, and place links don't expire.
> Think twice before sharing your home's link somewhere public.

## Where they're kept

Your places stay in this browser. [Syncing favourites](sync.md) can copy them to
another device; it never replaces a Home or Work that device already has. The
[Telegram bot](../bot/places.md) keeps its own.
