Service alerts cover what throws a bus journey off: trains, whose disruptions
send crowds onto buses and bring in free bridging buses, the roads the buses run
on, and the changes LTA and the operators announce ahead of time, in the
newsfeed.

## See what's happening now

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/service-alerts-now.png" alt="The Service alerts window: notification choices at the top, then Trains, with all train services running normally and LTA's notices, and Roads, counting the incidents across Singapore and listing those that can block or reroute a bus." width="906" height="697" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Trains and roads, as LTA reports them right now.</figcaption>
</figure>

Tap **Service alerts**, the bell in the Edge Tray. A window opens over the page, with three parts.

**Trains** says either that every train service is running normally, or, for
each line with a problem:

- whether it's disrupted or just delayed, and in which direction;
- the stations affected;
- where free buses and free shuttles are running, if LTA lists them;
- LTA's service notices, such as planned early closures.

**Roads** counts the traffic incidents LTA reports across Singapore. Those that
can block or reroute a bus, accidents, breakdowns, road blocks, diversions,
obstacles, fires and plant failures, are listed in full. The rest, such as heavy
traffic and roadworks, are counted by type; **Show the other** lists them too.

**Newsfeed** has what's been announced ahead of time: see [The newsfeed](#the-newsfeed).

Tap **Refresh** to fetch the latest. The window never shows a saved copy, so
without a connection it says it couldn't reach LTA instead. LTA's train feed
now and then doesn't answer; the app asks again a couple of times, and if
**Trains** still says it couldn't reach LTA while **Roads** is there, Refresh
in a moment. It's LTA, not your connection or an ad blocker.

While a train line is disrupted or delayed, a red bar along the top of the site
also says so, without opening the window: see [Disruptions bar](disruptions-bar.md).

The newsfeed is what LTA and the bus and train operators announce ahead of time,
which no live feed carries: roads closed for a run and the stops buses skip,
night closures for works, new services and route changes, and train service
adjustments. It's read every hour from LTA's announcements and from the news of
SBS Transit, SMRT, Tower Transit and Go-Ahead Singapore, the operators of every
bus and train line.

**Today and the next two weeks** lists LTA's notices that are on now or coming
up, soonest first, each with when it is (**Today**, **Tomorrow**, or its dates),
the buses it names, and a link to the notice itself. One that names one of your
[favourite](favourites.md) stops or buses says so, and comes first.

**From the operators** has their latest news, newest first, each linking to the
operator's own page.

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/timings-newsfeed-note.png" alt="Marina Bay Sands Theatre's timings, stop 03501, with a note under its name: Today: 97, 106, 133 skip this stop, first bus to 8.30am. Garmin Run Marathon Series Singapore 2026. Details." width="280" height="217" loading="lazy">

A stop's [timings](timings.md) say so too when buses skip it today or tomorrow,
which ones and for how long, read from the notice's own table of stops:
"Today: 97, 106, 133 skip this stop, first bus to 8.30am."

## Get notified

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/service-alerts-notify.png" alt="Notify me, even with the app closed: Train service alerts and Traffic alerts, each with Off, All updates and Disruptions only, and a note that notifications are off." width="878" height="217" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Train and traffic alerts are chosen separately.</figcaption>
</figure>

Under **Notify me, even with the app closed**, train and traffic alerts and the
newsfeed are chosen separately, each **Off** or one of two:

| | All updates | Disruptions only |
|---|---|---|
| **Train service alerts** (MRT and LRT) | Every change to train services, including new service notices. | A train disruption starting, changing or clearing. |
| **Traffic alerts** (roads and buses) | Every new traffic incident anywhere in Singapore. | New incidents that can block or reroute a bus: accidents, breakdowns, road blocks, diversions, obstacles, fires and plant failures. |

| | All news | Service changes only |
|---|---|---|
| **Newsfeed** (LTA and the operators) | Every new notice from LTA and every new piece of news from the operators. | New notices that change a bus or train service: diversions, closures, new and changed services, not fares or awards. |

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/alerts-newsfeed-modes.png" alt="Newsfeed (LTA and the operators), with Off, All news and Service changes only." width="310" height="119" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>The newsfeed, chosen like the others.</figcaption>
</figure>

So you can, say, hear about every train disruption but only the traffic that can
hold up a bus, and the newsfeed's service changes. New notices come in one
notification however many there are, as they're read every hour.

The first time, your browser asks whether the app may send notifications; allow
it. The note under the buttons then says what you'll be notified of.

> [!WARNING]
> Traffic **All updates** is busy. Singapore's roads report new incidents all
> day, so expect many notifications. **Disruptions only** is the quieter choice
> for most people.

Tapping a notification opens the app on the Service alerts window. A new alert
replaces the one before it in your notification tray rather than piling up.

### Which devices can get them

- **Android:** in Chrome, Edge or Samsung Internet, in a tab or as an installed
  app.
- **iPhone and iPad:** only from the app [added to your Home Screen](install.md#iphone-and-ipad),
  opened from there. Safari tabs can't receive notifications.
- **Computers:** while the browser is running, even with the tab closed.

### New versions of the app

In the [Android app](android-app.md#hear-about-new-versions) and the
[desktop app](desktop-app.md#updates), a third choice sits
under these two: **New versions of the app**, **Off** or **On**. On Android it
sends a notification the moment a new version is out, and is off until you
turn it on. On a computer it's on to start with, and says when the app has
found a new version. The website has no choice here, as it updates itself.

## Turn them off

Pick **Off** for both. The app tells the alerts server to forget this device. To stop the
browser asking again, you can also block notifications for sgbus.uwuapps.org in
your browser's site settings, and the app turns the setting off by itself the
next time it opens.

## Also in Telegram

The [Telegram bot](../bot/service-alerts.md) has the same three choices, with
