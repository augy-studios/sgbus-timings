Once you've [installed the app](install.md), you can open it straight at the
part you want. On Windows, you can also keep a stop's live timings in a widget.

## Shortcuts

Press and hold the app's icon on Android, or right-click it on Windows, macOS,
Linux or ChromeOS, for three shortcuts:

| Shortcut | Opens |
| --- | --- |
| **Stops near me** | The bus stops around you, with their timings. See [Find a bus stop](search.md). |
| **Navigate** | [Navigate by bus and train](nav.md), ready for where you're going. |
| **Service alerts** | [Service alerts](service-alerts.md): train disruptions and traffic incidents right now. |

On Android you can also drag a shortcut onto your home screen as an icon of its
own.

> [!NOTE]
> iPhone and iPad don't offer shortcuts for apps added to the Home Screen.

The widget shows the next buses at a stop you pick: every bus there, or only the
ones you choose. It's like a [routine](../bot/routines.md) in the Telegram bot,
kept on your screen.

### Add one

Widgets for web apps only work on **Windows 11**, for the app installed from
**Microsoft Edge**.

1. Install the app from Edge. See [Install it](install.md#windows-macos-linux-and-chromeos).
2. Open the **Widgets** board: press **Windows key + W**, or click the weather
   at the left of the taskbar.
3. Click **+**, find **Bus timings** under SG Bus Timings, and pin it.
4. Pick a stop: one of your [favourite stops](favourites.md), or type a stop's
   5-digit code.
5. Under **Buses**, type the bus numbers you want, separated by commas, such as
   `21, 22`. Leave it empty for every bus.
6. Click **Show timings**.

You can add as many as you like, each for a different stop or set of buses.

### Using it

- **Refresh** fetches the latest timings. The widget also updates itself when you
  open the Widgets board, and every so often in between, as often as Windows
  allows. **Updated** says when it last did.
- **Change** goes back to picking the stop and buses.
- **Open** opens the app at that stop.

Choose the widget's size from its **…** menu. Small shows the next two buses,
medium the next four, and large all of them, up to twelve.

> [!NOTE]
> **Android and iPhone:** a web app can't add widgets to your home screen; only
> apps from the Play Store or App Store can. See
> [What the web app can't do](../help/web-app-limits.md). For a stop's timings at a glance,
> try a [routine](../bot/routines.md) in the Telegram bot, or a
> [Stops near me shortcut](#shortcuts) on your home screen.
