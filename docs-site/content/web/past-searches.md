Tap into a search box and, until you type, it drops down what you've searched for
before, newest first: the last **24 hours**, then **Earlier**.

Tap one to use it again. Typing switches the box back to its usual suggestions;
emptying it brings the list back.

## Where they show

Every search box shares one list, and each shows what it can use:

| Box | Shows |
|---|---|
| The main search, for stops and buses | Stops, buses and postal codes |
| [Navigate](nav.md)'s **From** and **To** | Places, stations and stops |
| [Plan a route](route-planner.md)'s **Start** and **End** | Stops and postal codes |
| [Places](places.md)' **Where** | Places, stations and stops |

So a stop you searched for in the main box is there in Navigate's **To** next
time. A search is kept when you pick something in a box, not when you open a stop
some other way, and picking **Your location**, a saved place or a calendar event
isn't kept as one.

## The tabs

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| Clock | Your past searches |
| Star | Your saved [places](places.md), Home and Work first. In the main search, your favourite stops and buses instead. |
| Calendar | In Navigate only: your [calendar](calendar.md)'s events with a location |

## A search's menu

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/recents-menu.png" alt="Clarke Quay station's menu open in the list, with Save as place, Share and Remove." width="360" height="376" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>The menu beside a past search.</figcaption>
</figure>

Tap the three dots beside a search:

- **Save as place** opens [Places](places.md#save-a-new-place) with it filled in,
  to name and save.
- **Share** makes a "How to get to" link to it. See
  [Links you can share](links.md).
- **Remove** takes it off the list.

**Clear all**, at the foot of the list, empties it.

## Where they're kept

Past searches stay on this device, up to the last 50. They aren't sent anywhere,
but [Sync](sync.md) can copy them to another of your devices.
