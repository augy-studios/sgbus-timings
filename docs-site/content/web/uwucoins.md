Every trip you take with the [Get Off Alert](get-off-alert.md) earns
**uwuCoins**, and the coins buy spins of a wheel in Settings. Every spin wins
something.

## Earn them

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/tray-coins.png" alt="The uwuCoins button in the Edge Tray: a gold coin with uwu on it, and 230 on a gold badge." width="44" height="44" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>The coin in the Edge Tray, with how many you have.</figcaption>
</figure>

A trip earns coins once you've arrived, if the app saw you really take at least
one of its rides: your phone where the ride starts and where it ends, taking
about as long as the ride does. The more there is to the trip, the more it's
worth:

- 5 for getting there
- 1 for every 2 km you ride, up to 30
- 3 for each change
- 4 for each bus operator you ride with (SBS Transit, SMRT, Tower Transit,
  Go-Ahead), and 4 for each train operator (SMRT and SBS Transit)
- 2 for each MRT or LRT line

Some trips are feats, worth 100 more, once each: see
[Achievements](#achievements). A coin floats up from the bottom of the screen as they come in, and the coin
in the [Edge Tray](main-screen.md#pages-in-the-edge-tray) shows how many you
have. Tap it for the wheel. **uwuCoins** in Settings' **Edge Tray buttons** takes
it away.

## Trips from before uwuCoins

If you took trips before uwuCoins came, they earn their coins too, once. The
first time you open the app after this update, it looks through
[Your trips](trips.md) and gives each trip from before uwuCoins what it
would have earned, if you got there with the Get Off Alert and your location
saw you get on at least one of its rides. A trip taken with your location off
earns nothing. Your trips doesn't keep how far each ride went, so the distance
is worked out from how long you rode, at a bus's and a train's usual speed.

Those trips count towards the feats too: the buses and lines you rode on them
fill the bars in [Achievements](#achievements), and a trip of over three hours,
one back to where it started, or one down the old railway does that feat, each
worth 100 more. A window says how many trips counted, the coins they earned
and the feats done, or the celebration plays if you'd already ridden every MRT
and LRT line.

never counted twice, and nor are trips another device syncs to this one later.

## Spin the wheel

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/settings-coins.png" alt="uwuCoins in Settings, Help and extras: 230 uwuCoins, a gold wheel of eight slices, one with an egg and the others 20, 50, 30, 70, 40, 150 and 60 coins, a pointer at the top, Spin for 100, then how it works, 1 of 6 feats done with five still hidden, and 1 of 6 Easter eggs found." width="358" height="520" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>The wheel, in Settings' Help and extras.</figcaption>
</figure>

The wheel is in [Settings](settings.md), **Help and extras**, and it works like
gacha: a spin costs 100 uwuCoins and lands on one of eight slices at random: an **Easter egg** you haven't found
yet, or more coins, up to 150. Once you've found every egg, its slice is coins
too. Under the wheel, how many feats you've done and eggs you've found, and
**See your achievements**.

**Achievements** in [Settings](settings.md) lists the six feats: what each asks,
how far along you are, such as **6 of 9** lines ridden, and **Done** once it's
done. A feat's Easter egg comes from the wheel, not the feat, and says so once
you've found it.

## What an egg brings

Each egg unlocks a secret you haven't got yet, picked at random, and most also
bring a colour preset. One of them is **Your colours**, in the theme picker: one
colour, picked on a colour wheel or from your presets, and the app makes the
whole theme from it, the background, text, buttons and cards, in light and dark,
with all the text easy to read.
