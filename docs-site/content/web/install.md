title: Open and install the app
description: The web app runs in any modern browser, and installs onto your phone or computer like any other app.
---

Open [sgbus.uwuapps.org](https://sgbus.uwuapps.org) in your browser. There is
nothing to sign up for, and nothing to download first. Everything you save
stays in that browser.

- **The greeting** at the top greets you by the time of day, and by name once you have set
  one. Tap the title for [Settings](settings.md).
- **The Edge Tray** is the column of buttons at the top right of
  the screen. From the top: [**Favourites**](favourites.md) (the star),
  **Stops near me**,
  [**Navigate**](nav.md) (the arrowhead, with its **Bus only** tab for the
  [route planner](route-planner.md)), [**Service alerts**](service-alerts.md),
  [**Your trips**](trips.md) (the bar chart) and [**Places**](places.md) (the
  bookmark), then, in the [Android app](android-app.md#card-value) on a phone with
  NFC, **Card value** (the card), on a phone or tablet, [SimplyGo](#simplygo) (its own icon), the
  Telegram bot, a page to buy Augy a coffee, this guide (the question mark),
  [Settings](settings.md) (the gear) and the [theme picker](settings.md#themes).
  Hover over a button for its name, or, on a phone or tablet, press and hold it.
  The arrow beside the Edge Tray tucks it away at
  the edge of the screen, and brings it back. To have it on the left, see
  [Settings](settings.md).
- **The search box** finds a stop, a bus or an address. See
  [Find a bus stop](search.md). The [weather](weather.md) is under it, on the
  right.
- **The timings card** opens below once you pick a stop.

On a phone or tablet, press and hold any button, in the Edge Tray or elsewhere, to
see what it does, as hovering does with a mouse. Letting go doesn't press it. The
first time you open the app on a touch screen, a note beside the Edge Tray says
so.

### SimplyGo

On a phone or tablet, SimplyGo's icon in the Edge Tray goes to its app, for your
card's balance, top-ups and travel history.

- With the **SG Bus Timings Android app** installed, it opens SimplyGo straight
  away.
- Otherwise it opens SimplyGo's page on Google Play or the App Store: tap
  **Open** there if you have it, or **Install** if you don't. SimplyGo has no
  link a website can use to open its app.

## Install it

Installing puts SG Bus Timings on your home screen or in your app list, opening
in its own window without the browser's address bar. It also lets the app open
when you have no connection; see [Updates and offline use](updates.md). On
iPhone and iPad, it's also the only way to get
[service alert notifications](service-alerts.md#get-notified). Installed, it also
has [shortcuts and, on Windows, a widget](shortcuts-and-widgets.md).

### iPhone and iPad

1. Open the site in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

### Android

1. Open the site in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the menu, the three dots at the top right.
3. Tap **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**, then confirm.

#### Or the Android app

There's also an Android app (`sg-bus-timings-2.0.1.apk`): the same app, which
also follows your trip with the screen off, reads your EZ-Link or NETS FlashPay
card's value, reads your phone's calendar and opens SimplyGo itself. It's shared
as a file rather than on Google Play for now. See
[The Android app](android-app.md) to download and install it.

### Windows, macOS, Linux and ChromeOS

In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right of the address bar, or
open the browser menu and choose **Install SG Bus Timings**. Safari on a Mac
offers **File**, then **Add to Dock**.

#### Or the Windows and Linux app

There's also a desktop app for Windows (`.exe` or `.msi`) and Linux (`.deb` or
`.AppImage`): the same app in its own window, which stays in the tray and shows
Service alerts as desktop notifications. See
[Windows and Linux apps](desktop-app.md) to download and install it.

> [!NOTE]
> An installed app keeps its own favourites and settings, separate from the
> same site opened in a browser tab on some devices, especially iPhones. If
> your favourites seem to have vanished after installing, they are still in the
> browser. [Sync them](sync.md) across, or add them again.
