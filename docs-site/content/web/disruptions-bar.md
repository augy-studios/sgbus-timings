---
title: Disruptions bar
description: A red bar along the top of the site whenever an MRT or LRT line is disrupted or delayed.
---

While an MRT or LRT line is disrupted or delayed, a red bar appears along the top of
the site, under the update bar if there is one. It says which line, between which
stations, and in which direction, for example:

> Train disruption: East West Line, Tanah Merah to Pasir Ris, towards Pasir Ris

It shows only then. When LTA reports the line running normally again, the bar
goes by itself.

- **Details** opens the [Service alerts](service-alerts.md) card, with the stations
  affected and where free buses and shuttles are running.
- **Hide** puts the bar away until the disruption changes or you reload the page.

The site checks for disruptions every two minutes while it's open, and straight
away when you come back to it.

Traffic incidents don't bring the bar up: there's nearly always one somewhere in
Singapore. They're in the [Service alerts](service-alerts.md) card instead.
