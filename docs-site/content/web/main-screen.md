The app opens on **Glance**: the next bus in big letters, and your stops'
buses under it. **Bus timings**, the search box and a stop's timings, is the
page after it. Everything else is in the Edge Tray, each part of the app a page
of its own. Make any of them your home, and the app opens on it.

## Glance

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/glance.png" alt="Glance: a Saved stops chip and a round refresh button beside it, the next bus in big letters with when it comes and where, the weather, and under it one saved stop as a card with only the two favourite buses due there, each with where it is going and its next three times in minutes." width="280" height="464" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>The next bus, then your stops, with only your favourite buses.</figcaption>
</figure>

**Glance**, the eye at the top of the Edge Tray, is the page to read on the
way out:

- **The next bus**, in big letters: its number, how soon it comes
  (**Arriving** under a minute), where it's going, and the stop. A favourite
  bus comes first whenever one is coming.
- **Today's weather and air**, a tap away.
- **Your stops**: each a card, with one line per bus: its number, where it's
  going, and its next three times in minutes. **Arr** is under a minute away.
  The soonest bus is at the top.

Once you have [favourite buses](favourites.md#favourite-buses-are-pinned), the
cards show only those, and a stop none of them is due at is left out, so a
busy interchange is a line or two, not a dozen. With no favourite buses, every
bus shows. Star a bus by searching its number and tapping the star.

Tap the big bus, a line or a stop's name for its full [timings](timings.md),
on Bus timings, as a search would. **Back** comes back to Glance.

The times count down by themselves, and fetch again every 30 seconds while
the app is open on Glance. The round arrows beside the chip fetch them at once.

### Pick whose buses

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/glance-pick.png" alt="The chip over the next bus opened into a list: Saved stops, Nearby, Home, Work, Gym and Mum's." width="280" height="292" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>The chip picks the stops: saved, nearby, or near one of your places.</figcaption>
</figure>

The chip above it picks the stops:

| Chip | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Saved stops** | Your first four [favourite](favourites.md) stops. The one it starts on, once you've saved a stop. |
| **Nearby** | The four stops nearest where you are, each with how long the walk is. The first time, it asks to use your location. |
| **Home**, **Work** and your other [places](places.md) | Up to four stops within 500 m of the place, the place's own stop first if it's a bus stop. |
| Your [lists](favourites.md#lists) | The stops in the list, each with just the list's buses there, or every bus at a stop the list has no buses for. A list shows here once it has a stop or a bus at a stop in it. |

On **Nearby**, the big bus is the next one you'd make if you set off now,
and it says when to leave: **Leave in 3 min for**, or **Leave now for**. The
walk is a guess, the straight line with a quarter more for corners, as
[Will I make it?](timings.md#will-i-make-it) works it out.

The chip remembers what you picked. Turn Glance off in
[Settings](settings.md), **Next bus at a glance**: its button leaves the Edge
Tray, and the app opens on Bus timings instead.

## Bus timings

**Bus timings**, the bus under Glance in the Edge Tray, is the
[search](search.md) box and, once you pick a stop, its
[timings](timings.md). The **X** at the top of a stop's timings puts them away,
back to the search box.

## Pages in the Edge Tray

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/main-screen-tabs.png" alt="The app on a phone on Navigate: the Edge Tray first has its pages, the eye for Glance, Bus timings, Favourites, Navigate, which is filled in, Service alerts, Your trips and Places, over a line, with Stops near me and the rest below." width="390" height="844" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Navigate on show, its button filled in at the top of the Edge Tray.</figcaption>
</figure>

Each part of the app is a page of its own, with its button at the top of the
Edge Tray, over a line:

- **Glance**: the next bus, the weather and your stops' buses
- **Bus timings**: the search box, and a stop's timings
- **Favourites**
- **Navigate**, with its two tabs, **Bus and train** and **Bus only**, inside
  it. It opens on the one you used last.
- **Service alerts**
- **Your trips**
- **Places**

Tap a button to switch to its page; the one on show is filled in. Taps that
lead somewhere still take you there: a saved stop in Favourites opens its
timings on Bus timings, a place in Places opens the way there on Bus and train,
and a stop in a journey opens its timings. A bus's timings opened from a way in
Navigate have **Back to Navigate**, back to that same way.

The small things still open as windows over the page: Settings, the theme,
the weather, a bus's route, and the list of stops near you.

### Going back

**Back** (the Android app's back button, your browser's, or Telegram's) closes
the window that's open, then goes back to the page you were on before, one
step at a time. Only once you're back where you started, with nothing open,
does it leave the app. A window's **X**, or a tap outside it, still closes it
too.

In the Android app from version 2.2.7, Back closes what's open and then, on
whichever page you're on, says **Press back again to exit**: press it again
within two seconds to leave the app. It no longer steps back through the pages.

## Custom home

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/settings-main-screen.png" alt="Settings, Custom home, with Glance picked from Glance, Bus timings, Favourites, Navigate, Service alerts, Your trips and Places, and a note that it is the page the app opens on, first in the Edge Tray." width="396" height="295" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Custom home, in Settings.</figcaption>
</figure>

In [Settings](settings.md), **Custom home** picks the page the app opens on,
every time: **Glance** to begin with, or any of the others. Its button
moves to the top of the Edge Tray. Someone who mostly plans journeys can make
**Navigate** their home, and open straight on it, on the tab they used last.

A link to a stop, a bus or a journey still opens that.

## The tours

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/tour.png" alt="The tour's first step: the page dimmed, a light round the next bus, a chalk arrow to it, and the words Your next bus, big enough to read on the move. Tap it for all its times, with Next, Skip and the step dots." width="390" height="844" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>The tour's first step.</figcaption>
</figure>

The first time the app opens, a short tour shows the basics, one thing at a
time: the next bus, the chip and your stops on Glance, then the pages in the Edge
Tray and Settings. **Next** moves on, and **Skip** at the top ends it.

Every other page has a tour of its own too, shown the first time you open it:
Bus timings, Favourites, Navigate, Service alerts, Your trips and Places. Each
lights up what's on that page, such as Navigate's two tabs, From and To, and the
time to leave or arrive by. A tour leaves out what isn't there yet, so Your trips
with no trips just says how to start one.

<img src="/screenshots/web/tour-nav.png" alt="Navigate's tour, the first time it opens: the page dimmed, a light round its two tabs, Bus and train and Bus only, a chalk arrow to them, and the words Bus and train plans with the MRT too; Bus only keeps to buses, stop to stop. [Explorer](explorer.md) and the [sgbus terminal](terminal.md) have one each too,
the first time you open them: Explorer's lights up the dots round you, the
compass and Back, and the terminal's the prompt, where the answers show, and
how to close it.

To see any of them again, open [Settings](settings.md): **Show me around** has
a button for each page, for Explorer and for the sgbus terminal.
