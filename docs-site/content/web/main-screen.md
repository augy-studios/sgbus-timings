The app opens on **Bus timings**: the next bus in big letters, your stops
under the search box, and the search box itself. Everything else is in the
Edge Tray, each part of the app a page of its own. Make any of them your home,
and the app opens on it.

## The next bus at a glance

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/glance.png" alt="The top of Bus timings: a Saved stops chip, the next bus in big letters with when it comes and where, then the search box, and under it each saved stop as a card, one line per bus with where it's going and its next three times in minutes." width="280" height="1185" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>The next bus, then your stops, one line per bus.</figcaption>
</figure>

At the top of Bus timings:

- **The next bus**, in big letters: its number, how soon it comes
  (**Arriving** under a minute), where it's going, and the stop. A favourite
  bus comes first whenever one is coming.
- **Your stops**, under the search box: each a card, with one line per bus:
  its number, where it's going, and its next three times in minutes. **Arr**
  is under a minute away. The soonest bus is at the top.

Tap the big bus, a line or a stop's name for its full [timings](timings.md),
as a search would. The **X** at the top of those timings puts them away again.

The times count down by themselves, and fetch again every 30 seconds while
the app is open on Bus timings.

### Pick whose buses

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/glance-pick.png" alt="The chip over the next bus opened into a list: Saved stops, Nearby, Home, Work, Gym and Mum's." width="280" height="291" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>The chip picks the stops: saved, nearby, or near one of your places.</figcaption>
</figure>

The chip above it picks the stops:

| Chip | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Saved stops** | Your first four [favourite](favourites.md) stops. The one it starts on, once you've saved a stop. |
| **Nearby** | The four stops nearest where you are, each with how long the walk is. The first time, it asks to use your location. |
| **Home**, **Work** and your other [places](places.md) | Up to four stops within 500 m of the place, the place's own stop first if it's a bus stop. |

On **Nearby**, the big bus is the next one you'd make if you set off now,
and it says when to leave: **Leave in 3 min for**, or **Leave now for**. The
walk is a guess, the straight line with a quarter more for corners, as
[Will I make it?](timings.md#will-i-make-it) works it out.

The chip remembers what you picked. Turn the whole glance off in
[Settings](settings.md), **Next bus at a glance**.

## Pages in the Edge Tray

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/main-screen-tabs.png" alt="The app on a phone on Bus only: the Edge Tray's first seven buttons are the pages, Bus timings, Bus only, which is filled in, Favourites, Bus and train, Service alerts, Your trips and Places, over a line, with Stops near me and the rest below." width="390" height="844" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Bus only on show, its button filled in at the top of the Edge Tray.</figcaption>
</figure>

Each part of the app is a page of its own, with its button at the top of the
Edge Tray, over a line:

- **Bus timings**: the next bus, your stops, the search box, and a stop's timings
- **Favourites**
- **Bus and train** and **Bus only**, Navigate's two tabs
- **Service alerts**
- **Your trips**
- **Places**

Tap a button to switch to its page; the one on show is filled in. Taps that
lead somewhere still take you there: a saved stop in Favourites opens its
timings on Bus timings, a place in Places opens the way there on Bus and train,
and a stop in a journey opens its timings.

The small things still open as windows over the page: Settings, the theme,
the weather, a bus's route, and the list of stops near you.

### Going back

**Back** (the Android app's back button, your browser's, or Telegram's) closes
the window that's open, then goes back to the page you were on before, one
step at a time. Only once you're back where you started, with nothing open,
does it leave the app. A window's **X**, or a tap outside it, still closes it
too.

## Custom home

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/settings-main-screen.png" alt="Settings' Custom home, with Bus timings picked from Bus timings, Favourites, Bus and train, Bus only, Service alerts, Your trips and Places, and a note that it's the page the app opens on, first in the Edge Tray." width="396" height="295" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Custom home, in Settings.</figcaption>
</figure>

In [Settings](settings.md), **Custom home** picks the page the app opens on,
every time: **Bus timings** to begin with, or any of the others. Its button
moves to the top of the Edge Tray. Someone who only plans bus routes can make
**Bus only** their home, and open straight on it.

A link to a stop, a bus or a journey still opens that.

## The tour

<figure>
<img src="/screenshots/web/tour.png" alt="The tour's first step: the page dimmed, a light round the next bus, a chalk arrow to it, and the words Your next bus, big enough to read on the move. Tap it for all its times, with Next, Skip and the step dots." width="390" height="844" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>The tour's first step.</figcaption>
</figure>

The first time the app opens, a short tour shows the basics, one thing at a
time: the next bus, the chip, the search box, your stops, the pages in the
Edge Tray and Settings. **Next** moves on, and **Skip** at the top ends it.

To see it again, open [Settings](settings.md) and tap **Show me around**.

All of this is in the web app, so the Android and desktop apps have it too,
with no update needed.
